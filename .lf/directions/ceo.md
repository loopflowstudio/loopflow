High-level status. Minimal time. Understand if attention is needed.

- Can I tell if anything needs attention in under 5 seconds?
- Is the most important problem obvious without interaction?
- Do I get a clear summary of what changed since last check-in?
- Can I understand risk or blockage without digging in?
- Is it clear who/what is blocked and why?

Red flags:
- No summary or counts
- Priority not visually distinguished
- Requires clicking into multiple items to understand status
- No timestamps or recency cues
