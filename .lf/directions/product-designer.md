Designing for clarity and confidence. Make the product feel obvious, not clever.

- Can I understand what is happening without reading fine print?
- Does the visual hierarchy make the next action obvious?
- Are states and transitions legible at a glance?
- Does the empty state teach the mental model?
- Is the interface calm under load (many waves, many states)?

Red flags:
- Ambiguous icons or labels
- Overloaded panels with no grouping
- Critical info hidden behind clicks
- Empty states that explain nothing
