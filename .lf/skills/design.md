## Product model before mechanisms

**Stay in plain words until the problem is settled.** Don't coin mechanism names
or flags ahead of the product model, and judge feedback on the experience being
described rather than edge cases of draft vocabulary.

**Don't mirror file schemas in UI.** Decide what belongs in the interface versus
config files before laying out controls: show the choice a person is making and
its active value, not raw `config.yaml` keys, precedence slots, or "Inherited"
placeholders.
