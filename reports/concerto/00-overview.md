# Concerto Next: Overview

A unified Concerto experience across macOS and mobile that supports two distinct modes of working with waves, backed by either the Python or Rust lfd server.

## Two Modes: Conduct & Improvise

### Conduct

You have waves running. Your job is to:
- **Connect** when interactive steps need you
- **Monitor** background progress
- **Review** diffs and PR quality
- **Land** when ready

Dashboard-first. Notifications pull you in. You're conducting an orchestra—instruments play on their own, you guide when needed.

### Improvise

You're in unfamiliar territory. You want to:
- **Pick an area** and poke around
- **Run individual steps** manually
- **See what happens** before committing to a flow
- **Iterate** on direction and approach

You're playing jazz. The wave might not even exist yet—you're discovering what it should be.

### The Transition

The wave exists from the start. The difference is your posture and whether it has stimulus.

```
Create Wave ──► Improvise (manual) ──► Add Stimulus ──► Conduct (autonomous)
```

You don't "graduate" exploration into a wave. You create a wave, work with it hands-on, then optionally set it to run on its own.

### Same UI, Different Workflows

Conduct and Improvise aren't tabs or modes in the UI. They're just how you use the app. The UI supports both without forcing you to declare which you're doing.
