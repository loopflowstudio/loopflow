requires: a Task brief
produces: a chosen direction recorded in the design, with the visions it was chosen from
action_style: exploratory

Show several distinct visions of the outcome so the choice can be "this one, build it."

Read the Task, `scratch/` and what people have already said. An existing design
or a suggested approach is one candidate, not the answer. Read enough of the
system to make each vision concrete and to know what it would cost.

## Dream three visions

Each vision is a coherent world, not a presentation variant: they differ by
mechanism or by the experience they promise. At least one is radical success,
what this would be if it worked far beyond the brief. Give each a short title,
one line on what's true in that world, and its cost and risk beside its
appeal.

Three is inspiration, not a rule. A Task with one obvious answer says so and
spends the time on its open question. Two visions or five can beat three.

## Make them tangible

Build one page, `scratch/explore.html`, with a panel per vision. For anything
people see or touch: real-looking content and the key interaction working. For
other work, the form that shows the difference: CLI transcripts, data-model
sketches, a day in the life of the system, the failure each one avoids.

The page is what gets reviewed, so make it good. One self-contained file, no
network, readable in light and dark and at narrow widths. Lead with the
decision needed. Show the thing, not a description of it, and mark proposed
apart from existing. One reading column, no dashboard chrome. End with numbered
questions so "2b" is a complete answer. Say its path, and regenerate the same
file each round.

## Follow their lead

The first page is an opening move, not a ballot. The answer may be one
outright, A + B, or "three more, keep X fixed and vary Y", for many rounds.
Answer each request as given. Don't steer toward closure, summarize a choice
not yet made, or turn a remark into a decision.

Write into the design what will be built, with the exact words people used
beside it and the speaker's name. "Looks fine" stays "looks fine". Keep open
threads open. The page is a view; the decision lives in the Markdown design.
After "build this", write the chosen direction there for planning and link the
latest page.

## Headless

Produce the visions and the page, recommend one, and leave the choice open in
`scratch/questions.md`.
