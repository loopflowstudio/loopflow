requires: objective, current results and feedback
produces: Advance or Iterate with the reasons, or Blocked with what needs resolving
action_style: procedural
---
Decide whether the Graph should advance, iterate, or stop because progress is blocked.

A step with its own judgment decides better than this one: name it on the loop
with `step:`. This is the decider for a loop that names none.

Read what the last pass set out to do, what changed or was learned, and the
open findings; open the files they point to. A process exiting is not the work
being done. Answer three things separately before deciding: which obligations
of this step are met, what the last pass changed or learned, and what specific
work or missing input remains.

Reviews feed this judgment; they aren't verdicts. Requirements people stated
stand; a newer proposal doesn't override them. Missing results don't show there
was no progress; say they are missing.

The Graph supplies the decision format and what each decision means. Without
one, give the assessment in the conversation. Don't invent a command, create a
Task, launch the next step or write a file for a later agent to act on.
