---
requires: a Wave or the repository to look at
produces: dated findings, actions taken, or a no-action result
action_style: procedural
---
Reconcile purpose with what the whole system is learning.

Edit `.lf/wave/` only when the running Graph says to; otherwise put proposed
Wave changes in `scratch/`.

Work the requested scope: a Wave and its Tasks, or the repository and its Waves.
When `LOOPFLOW_GRAPH_NAME` is `vsm-operate`, default to the whole repository even
inside a Task or Wave; only the request narrows it. For repository scope, read
`lf wave list --json`, then `lf wave status <wave> --json` and
`lf wave show <wave> --json`. Read earlier passes' findings. If the scope is
missing, say so; never substitute another repository or Wave.

Read the stated purpose, scope and commitments next to the delivery,
coordination, capacity and outside findings. Work bottom-up: a Task can
challenge its Wave's premise, and a Wave can challenge the repository's
direction. No single level defines identity.

Hold competing readings open until facts or a decision settle them. Tell a
day-to-day tradeoff from a real change of purpose; don't keep a stale purpose by
default or rewrite it to chase one signal. Apply accepted decisions where they
already live; anything beyond accepted direction stays a proposal naming the
decision needed.

Carry each decision, its source and what it changes back to the affected Waves
and Task briefs or conversations. Keep dissent and the findings that raised it.
A proposal nobody received is not a decision.

Date your sources and keep observations apart from guesses. A stale or failed
read is unknown, not finished or idle. Tasks keep progressing on their own and
reviews stay where they are. No action is a fine result; don't manufacture work.

Reply with findings, what you did and what happened, and open questions. In a
Graph, keep one `scratch/` note keyed by `LF_GRAPH_ID` across S1–S5 for scope,
sources and disagreements. Plan changes are proposals. Check that writes landed;
a draft or failed post is still pending.
