Assess environmental changes.

## Goal

Filter the s4 scan into relevant, prioritized environmental pressure.

Separate urgent changes from informational ones. Identify which waves should
prepare, react, or ignore the signal.

## Workflow

1. Read `scratch/vsm-s4-scan.md`.
2. Judge relevance, urgency, owning wave, and likely impact.
3. Distinguish urgent action from preparation work.
4. Produce proposals grounded in the environmental evidence.

## Output

Write `scratch/vsm-s4-assessment.md`:

```markdown
# VSM S4 Assessment — <date>

## Summary
<what changed outside the chord and why it matters>

## Findings
| Finding | Urgency | Owning wave | Impact |
|---------|---------|-------------|--------|
| ... | urgent | ... | ... |

## Proposals
- <backlog or mutation proposal driven by the environment>

## Watchlist
<important but not yet urgent changes>
```

## What to avoid

**Unsourced urgency.** Every finding should point back to a real external signal.
