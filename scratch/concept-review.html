Normal path, failure, recovery

Close A → close B → Undo B keeps B’s history and eventually reclaims A. A shell close or pane hide can also displace A’s Undo entry. A still-hidden pane keeps its own pages. Failed navigation, WebContent death and workspace loss must permit reclamation without waiting forever.

Pending WebKit work can fault after native destruction. Navigation completion, two animation frames and a retention deadline acknowledge no drained layer tree. Relaunch restores access; live page history after process death is not promised. Missing crash evidence stays unknown.
