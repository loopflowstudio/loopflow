The mismatch: a late wait can hide a dispatched retry

Existing projection: dispatch → delayed worker's wait → “retryable”
Proposed projection: dispatch + later wait → original attempt still pending
                     + correlated completion → continued

Built: a fixed dispatch key prevents sending that failed turn twice. The latest worker snapshot still controls the reading and can lose the request identity. Proposed: derive the reading from that turn's retained facts; keep changing wait information separate. Reuse Session history and the account broker.

Typing must take precedence

A history read followed by a send leaves a race. Codex also hands off credentials before its second read; the earlier native probe showed active-response handoff can fail that turn. Claude can queue an old continuation behind newer input. Next implementation: demonstrate admission at the native operation, including credential handoff. Another idle check or a prompt instruction is insufficient.

Proposed usage text: “Checking again does not resend a dispatched retry. Its progress follows the original attempt, even after a reconnect. New input takes precedence over recovery of an older turn.”
