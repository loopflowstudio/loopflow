One rule, one place

Built. The command decides. Cli::names_work (launches) and resumes_work (resumes, through work_flag) are true when the command names its Work or is a step of a Task's run. That sets AgentConfig::managed; nothing reads agent_sessions.work_source to decide it, and the column is still written.

Looked at and left alone

A Session link stays bare even when its Session belongs to a Task: the link names a conversation, not Work, and the rule is the command. Desktop runs beside its server, so the missing check costs little there.
