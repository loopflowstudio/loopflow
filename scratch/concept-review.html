Failure and recovery

CaseBehavior already implemented
Headless refusal, bare or TaskTry configured model/harness order; keep receipts and seed history when changing harness. Exhaustion stops with the provider error and next-step advice.
Interactive refusal, bare or TaskKeep provider, thread and refusal visible. No automatic follow-up input. Retry deliberately or use lf -a claude resume <session-id>.
Old isolated transcript missingReport missing history; retain stored attribution and original thread selection. Never copy credentials or silently substitute a new thread.
Remote native login missingDestination client reports its login error. Sign in there, then launch again; no Claude/Codex credential transfer.

A bare harness pin keeps that harness; bare Claude may try its other models. A model pin stays on that model. Quota snapshots and successful answers mentioning limits do not authorize fallback.
