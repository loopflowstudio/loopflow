Doctor should show every recent bad exit, and search the five minutes after the last heartbeat

The consequential mismatch: one line per bundle hides repeats

On 2026-10-29 Desktop called exit(1) at 07:27, and again at 08:00. With the implemented reader, only the 08:00 exit appears. The accepted live acceptance (quit with ⌘Q, then Force Quit, then “lf doctor names both”) cannot pass.

Implemented

⚠ desktop-exit  Loopflow 0.15.1 (pid 9446) ended without a record between 09:10 and 08:02;
                the system log is searched only over 15 minutes

Proposed (matches the accepted Demo)

⚠ desktop-exit  Loopflow 0.15.1 (pid 9446) was killed by SIGKILL (Force Quit, kill -9, or the system) before 09:16, 1h12m after launch
                Loopflow 0.15.1 (pid 8811) quit 09:01:40: Quit requested by Dock (pid 612)

Rule: per bundle, list the abnormal ends from the last 24 h, newest first, with at most three lines and then “… and N earlier”. Then add the most recent end if it is not already listed. A running launch still says “running since”.

Second: the heartbeat already bounds the search

DesktopVitals writes every 300 s from its own utility queue, which keeps running when the main thread hangs. A launch therefore died within about one interval of its last desktop= line. Today the window ends at the next launch. When that launch comes more than 15 minutes later, doctor refuses to search.

window = [last line, next launch or now]      # refused when longer than 15 min
window = [last line, min(next launch or now, last line + 300 s + 60 s)]   # always searched

This deletes the refusal branch and its message. A Force Quit at night followed by a relaunch in the morning still names SIGKILL.

Kept

The journal path under timings/ stays, because timings.py reads it and a rename buys nothing. Quit still reports ok, and the check never fails doctor.
