Return, failure, recovery

Normal: returning before cleanup keeps execution; leaving starts a new grace. Returning after closure continues saved provider history without reopening the Task. The saved screen is not a restored editor; input is never replayed.

Failure: a failed store read cannot authorize closure. Viewer writes retry off the input path. Failed checkout cleanup retries from its existing queue; reopening cancels that intent. An older owner may miss the notice but still receives the verified signal.

Counterexample retained: return between cleanup’s decision and signal, and the conversation can end in front of the viewer with “Task completed”. Resume continues history. The direct-signalling choice includes this race.
