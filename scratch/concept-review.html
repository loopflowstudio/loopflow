Advance. No concept change needed.

The contract answers two questions: what is here? with a reading, and what just happened? with an event. Recovery replaces state without replaying reactions.

Action → conceptBefore: readings onlyAfter: implemented API
Open a Task → current stateRender a complete reading.subscribe(task.get) supplies the baseline; old occurrences stay quiet.
Send direction → immutable steerObserve the updated thread.task.steer carries the steer and original comment when present.
Land a PR → recorded mergeObserve changed delivery state.task.shipped identifies the merged PR, including when the Task leaves the plan.
Finish a step → recorded exitObserve Graph progress.graph.step_finished carries the step and outcome, including failure.
The main trap: appending twice. A changed reading arrives before its events. A comment may already be visible. Use its comment ID to append once; use the event to react. Connection seq orders delivery, not occurrence identity or historical causality. Overlapping subscriptions can describe the same occurrence.
