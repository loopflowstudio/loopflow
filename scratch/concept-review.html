Keep the design; advance to verification.

No open product choice. The branch implements an owner’s final reason without adding a Session lifecycle or a recovery service.

Biggest mismatch resolved: a bridge disappearing used to look the same whether execution finished or its owner was replaced. A final Close separates those outcomes. Bare EOF still means unknown.

One interaction, including failure

A viewer receives replaced, keeps its screen and requests a new attachment by Session. The successor has fresh stream and control claims. Another viewer’s input claim remains theirs. After execution finishes, finished stops reconnection.

Counterexample: replacement launch can fail after Close. The reason announces intent, not readiness. Bounded attachment reports unavailable; it cannot launch a provider. A slow or broken connection can lose Close entirely, so its EOF cannot become finished.

Close is transient and outside counted terminal bytes. The server and raw relay forward it; Desktop’s decoded relay presents the ending and stops recovery. Desktop does not automatically follow a replacement.

Checks for the next step

  1. Does the model need another state? No: owner reason, Session identity and existing recovery cover the normal and failed paths.
  2. What remains to show? Production resume/rollover and Desktop’s decoded ending. Recorded passing tests use a manually supplied successor and do not establish those paths.
