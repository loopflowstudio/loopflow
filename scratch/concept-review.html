Leaving a conversation keeps it running

No further design decision needed for this change. The lifetime and input deletions match the working design. Advance to gate and demo; suspension remains deferred.

Built from scratch/process-leaks.md, scratch/questions.md, docs/sessions.md, swift/README.md, rust/loopflow/src/transport/{input,lifetime}.rs, store/sqlite/sessions.rs, ops/task/retirement.rs and builtins/task/skill/demo.md (the last three under rust/loopflow/src/).

The mismatch removed

Before: quiet conversations were meant to expire, but an edited draft could hold them indefinitely—even after their Task finished. Built on this branch: open conversations stay resident; Task closure ends them through cleanup. No keystroke ledger decides their lifetime.

Use it, lose the connection, return

Leave a draft in an open Task and close its viewer. Return to the same provider. Complete the Task and leave again: after fifteen minutes without viewing, cleanup can end it once live work settles. A returning viewer cancels cleanup.

A locked store cannot stall typing or authorize closure. The owner retries the judgment after recovery without replaying input. After cleanup, provider history and a saved screen remain; the editor and deleted checkout do not return.

Counterexample kept: a missed hook can hide an active turn. Output arriving after the cleanup judgment invalidates that judgment. The output check remains; the idle timer is gone.

Words people will meet

“A conversation stays running while its scope is open.” — current docs/sessions.md

The demo skill now waits for live work, quits each demo provider and joins its processes before removing the runtime root. Quitting the app alone is insufficient. History stays.

Built: Wave and repository conversations follow the open-Task rule. Bare owners also remain until quit during this transition. The ignored end_detached_after key and unused database column remain only for release compatibility and staged removal.

Proposed, unbuilt: bare-direct launch and resident-limit suspension. LOO-824 supplies the planned emulator; reading an empty editor from it has not been chosen. Nothing here adds a replacement ledger.
