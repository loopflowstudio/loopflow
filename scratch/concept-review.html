The mismatch that matters

When the passive viewer existed, a tab opened in the background held the worktree: “Hidden or unfocused viewers count, passive ones too”. The November 5 simplification removed that viewer, and Task & workflow took its slot without a hold. Three routes now produce a tab with no Conversation:

  • A repository or Wave opening, for a Task with no running provider.
  • A newly started Task arriving in the Workspace tab.
  • An agent running lf open on its own Task, though that agent's own viewer usually holds it.

So: open a Wave, work in a Task's shell, the PR merges and the Task completes. Fifteen minutes after the first cleanup sweep, the worktree and its uncommitted files are deleted under that shell. Uncommitted files not stopping cleanup is accepted. A person not being told is the part to fix.

What changes

Proposed for docs/workspaces.md, prose only:

A Conversation pane keeps a completed Task's worktree, focused or not. Fifteen minutes after the last one closes, LF deletes a merged Task's worktree and any uncommitted files in it. Requested work finishes first. Task & workflow and shells do not keep the worktree, so run lf open --task <task> before you finish work in a tab without a Conversation. Task and conversation history remain.

Checked and left alone

  • Host routing. The current viewer outranks startup environment, and a stale Rex placement is refused.
  • A running conversation that is not the Task's primary. A broad open shows Task & workflow. The pane judges the Session it names.
  • Two broad opens, two followers. Each session is its own view of the same Work.
