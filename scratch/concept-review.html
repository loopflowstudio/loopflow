One start, one receipt, the same conversation

Terminal or Desktop starts the same Task. Lose the reply after registration: the Process, creation key, native thread and pending input survive. Ending the request cannot end or repeat detached work. Resume retains the captured Graph; aliases, abbreviations and global flags still identify landing commands.

A watch that falls behind requests a fresh reading. An unavailable scope keeps its previous membership. Rental recovery retains its effects and warnings; command labels follow invocation attribution. No new dispatcher, saved identity, Graph field or application-services framework.

Build and recover

scripts/dev-lf --version refreshes roots before building. The existing daemon reloads on natural idle exit; bare Cargo does not refresh roots. Cache failure permits direct compilation. For local debug sources: scripts/dev-lf --isolated-build --version.

The experimental cache remains a prerequisite for demonstrated cross-root reuse. Root upkeep alone validates neither production cache selection nor speedup.
