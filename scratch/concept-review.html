Normal path, failure, recovery

Settled input. Review ends with nothing pending. Stop verified owners, confirm exit, remove the runtime root, reopen the same history.

Unsettled input. The demo records it and releases the root but stops nothing, because the 24h minimum for owner termination still stands. On your Machine, writing the marker restarts the cleaner’s newest-write window; a demo with an unknown outcome waits seven days. After that the draft may be lost with its owner. History survives.

Interrupted teardown. The runtime root stays, and the note names survivors and the next action. Nothing is deleted underneath a surviving owner.

Abandoned review. Nothing releases the root. Its runtime stays valid and the cleaner reports it retained. That is visible, and it is not the incident’s path-loss condition, but it does not reclaim anything.

Idle, replaced and superseded owners keep LOO-709’s protection. Only authorized cleanup of a released root ends a draft-holding owner. The marker stays out of the builtin skill: the cleaner is machine-local, so repository guidance under .lf/ names it.
