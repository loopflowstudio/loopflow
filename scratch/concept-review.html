Failure: the recorded attachment was removed in Linear. The update fails, IssueAttachments doesn't list it, LinkAttachment makes a new one, and its id becomes the next record. Recovery from a lost first reply: Linear answers "already linked", the read finds this issue's attachment for the URL, the action confirms. Linked to another issue: the action retires with Linear's message.
