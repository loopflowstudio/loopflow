When the recovery row goes away

Outcome: the design changed; iterate to implement. The five states hold. The row has no end, so doing what it asks turns it into a warning that stays. Direction: the row ends at your next message in that conversation. No open product choice; the direction is agent-written and reversible.

The mismatch

Built now. A conversation that was open before this version, after its login runs out:

Send it again yourself

You send it again. Within a minute, and from then on:

Newer input or incomplete history; continue from the conversation

The same happens when you type over a Waiting row, where the guide says "your message wins and the old turn is dropped". Continued stays above the pane through every later turn. Review conversation stays after you have read the end and carried on. The reading is the thread's latest failed turn for as long as the thread lives; only another limit or a new thread replaces it.

Every conversation open at install is unguarded, so this is the first thing the row does.

"Newer input" stops being a reason for Review conversation. That state keeps earlier work, incomplete history, and an unconfirmed or cancelled resend.

Normal, failure, recovery

Normal: Waiting → Recovering → Continued → your next message → no row.

Failure: a hand resend rejected again is a new failed turn with its own row, as built.

Counterexamples the implementation must hold:

  • An unconfirmed resend that arrives late is Loopflow's turn, not yours; it must not clear its own Review conversation row.
  • A message queued behind a running resend must not end the row while the resend runs.
  • An older Claude client may report no turn start; the transcript's newer user row is the fallback.
  • A row that ended is never requested, checked or resent again; dispatch stays in Session history.

Also settled

One wording for one condition. Claude says "No eligible login is ready for this model"; Codex and the guide say "has capacity". Both become "is ready": a model resting after its failure has capacity by the poll and still cannot be selected.
