Built from lf account --help, provider_account.rs, LOO-593 scratch/api-engine.md decision 10, your website mock, and the tools listed under “What we take”

Decision needed first: switching at every launch moves every open Claude conversation between accounts. Anthropic documents that Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 thinking blocks stay with the account that produced them, so moved conversations can lose earlier reasoning on those models. Do you want a limit on how often the Machine switches (question 1), or should Task workers get their own credentials so your open Sessions stop moving (question 2)?

Anthropic's terms

Anthropic's legal and compliance page says developers “may not collect, store, or intermediate Claude.ai credentials or session tokens”, and that Pro, Max and Ultra limits “assume ordinary, individual usage”. lf stores logins and sends access tokens to rentals. This isn't a legal conclusion, and nothing here settles it, but it bears more on shipping this to other people than on your own accounts.

Questions

  1. How often should the Machine switch?
    1. Every launch, as built
    2. At most every 5 minutes, and only when the top account clearly beats the active one
  2. Should Task workers keep their own credentials so open Sessions stop moving?
    1. No, everything shares the login
    2. Probe CLAUDE_SECURESTORAGE_CONFIG_DIR first with fake tokens, then decide
  3. lf account use:
    1. Holds until the account fills up or use auto
    2. Lasts one launch, as built
  4. This CLI work:
    1. All in #1828, no stacking
    2. #1828 takes the correctness fixes; the CLI gets its own Task
  5. lf account as shown: what would you change? pause, next and the fix-it lines are the parts most worth a reaction.
