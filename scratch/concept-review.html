Notice: one slot, two kinds

Built, a new Notice replaces the current one. Proposed, events replace events; conditions stay underneath:

away posted        →  shows "Machine is away"
Complete refused   →  shows "LOO-4 couldn't be completed: its PR #12 is open"
six seconds later  →  shows "Machine is away" again
Machine returns    →  clear(key: "away") → nothing shown

Without this, any event posted while away hides "away" for good.
