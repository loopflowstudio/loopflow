Still five. The requirement "Distinguish rejected turns from uncertain delivery/completed effects" is why the no-guard case does not fold into Review conversation: there the turn is known rejected and safe to send again.

Normal, failure, recovery

Normal: guarded conversations read Waiting → Recovering → Continued, as built.

Failure: an unguarded conversation with no capacity anywhere is resent by hand and rejected again. That is a new failed turn with its own row.

Recovery: Codex reads its hooks live, so a later check may find the guard and take the turn if nothing newer was typed. The server therefore keeps checking manual_retry rows and the button stays. A late wait still cannot replace dispatch.

Not directed

Desktop picks the affected Sessions from the ones it has loaded, with its own copy of can_retry; the served request could own "every waiting conversation". No failure observed.
