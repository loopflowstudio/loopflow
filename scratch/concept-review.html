A Task on the phone and on the Mac

Concept review of Task e166ff, before anything is built. 2026-11-22.

The area taken. The plan moves the iPhone app to the shared type ramp and the shared Task row, workflow line, button and state view. Its outcome is that someone "sees the same Task row, status word, workflow sentence" on both. So the area is a Task as a person meets it on either surface: the word on its row, the sentence about where it stands, and the action that moves it. The ramp, spacing, pairing and New Task parts of the plan were read too and stand as written.

Outcome of this review. The plan changed. It would have drawn the same row around different words. Four things were added so the row, the sentence and the action agree across surfaces; none needs Rust.

The biggest mismatch: the status word

Walk it. A Task's agent is working. On the Mac its row says Busy. You pick up the phone, and under the plan as kickoff wrote it you see the same row, same faces, same spacing, and the word Running. The agent finishes its turn: the Mac says Available, the phone says Ready. A Task between runs says "Idle" on the Mac and "Open" on the phone. Sharing the view made the difference easier to see, not smaller.

RunAuto-Ship in "Task actions"

The sentence wraps in full; the Mac's 34 pt bar limits it to one line from outside. runAction moves to TaskGraph in the shared library and both surfaces call it. The phone subscribes to the Task over its own link, as the Mac does. The phone says "Auto-Ship" and "Stop Auto-Ship".

The example sentences here are illustrations of the shape. The one probed on this Task was "plan is running kickoff. Auto stands in next at ship-review." (66 characters; about 40 fit an iPhone SE at 17 pt).

The state view

Before (plan as kickoff wrote it)

Every state: a serif title, one sentence, buttons. "No changes yet" "gets its sentence", and so would "The Session ended." and three more that are one line today.

After

The sentence and buttons are optional. A one-line state keeps its line, word for word, as the title. A state with a title and a reason keeps both. "Disconnected" is the only new title, over the reason the link gives.

Outside this work

The row's status written once, in Rust. The Mac builds its word, the time it began and the ask from Session records in Swift; the phone builds its word from the list's states in Swift. After this Task they agree by sharing two label tables and a test. The whole fix is for the list reading to carry each Task's status (word, tone, since, ask) and for both rows to print it, deleting SessionProgramStatus.label and the mapping added here. It takes a wire field, DTO fixtures in both languages and a Mac migration. Not filed.
