4. Choices made for review & scope boundaries

  • choice made Top-level step in every C1 chip label: Use <Status>: <top> · <inner> when top != inner (e.g., Paused: build · implement, Pausing: deliver · land, Run: build · implement, Waiting: deliver · land) and <Status>: <top> when top-level or at start (Paused: plan). Passed over omitting the top-level step because C1 replaced the top-level step button with the chip.
  • choice made WorkflowAction::Pausing on the wire: Add a dedicated pausing action rather than overloading resume/unpause + roadmap.runControl.pausing. Passed over leaving it split because the split caused the ⏸ → ▶ glyph flip and required two subscriptions to agree.
  • choice made Advance cursor when a draining cmd: step exits 0: Passed over keeping the cursor on the drained step because non-idempotent commands would execute twice on unpause --run and the Graph log misrepresented exit 0 as interrupted.
  • choice made Reselect Task on Mac Complete → Undo & move iPhone ··· to nav toolbar: Both directly fix flow regressions spotted in the C1 QA walkthrough while keeping Mac’s bar free of ···.
  • Outside LOO-1078 (separate outcomes): (1) Holistic session-vs-worker availability semantics (paused sidebar rows showing Available · inferred from the open primary conversation); (2) Separate-session Codex 0.171.0 tool jobs (osPid: null in app-server); (3) Pause issued in the narrow window after a move payload is sent and before the destination checkout exists; (4) Pre-existing task_complete leaving a running Graph step alive until it exits, and pre-existing raw step UUID in task_execution.rs:336 (#1974).
