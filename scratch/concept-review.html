Proposed usage: Move to a Wave or No Wave. Cached lists update immediately; the conversation and drafts stay open. Offline changes wait for their original Machine. Refusal restores only the refused change and leaves later edits and navigation alone.

Field incorporation and answer completeness are separate. Fresh parent data cannot prove fresh activity or content. Client-derived answers never become Machine ReadingStamps. Complete → Reopen, A → B → C, partial content, and closed-query restart must pass on both clients before Move migrates.
