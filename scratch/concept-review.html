Biggest mismatch in the current patch

Synthetic counterexample, not a captured provider response:

{"rate_limit_type":"seven_day", "model":"fable",
 "status":"rejected", "utilization":1.0}

Existing patch: the reported model wins → Fable-only window → Sonnet remains eligible despite aggregate exhaustion.

Proposed correction: known global bucket wins → global hold → every model waits. For seven_day_fable, the named Fable family wins even if incidental metadata says Sonnet. For seven_day_overage_included, the reported or launched family supplies the scope, as the working design requires.

Normal use, failure, recovery

Normal: refresh Fable to 11% → replace its old alias → Fable and Sonnet can launch. At 100%, only Fable waits through reset grace.

Failure: an aggregate weekly rejection blocks all models. A Fable-only reading cannot release it. Unknown causes and paid-credit holds survive unrelated included-quota readings.

Recovery: a matching aggregate reading with room releases the global hold. If a rejection arrives during refresh, its revision wins; status shows the saved result and omits the discarded reading from verified windows.
