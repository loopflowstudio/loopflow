Normal, failed and recovered

A normal answer completes once. A quota snapshot or answer prose mentioning limits does not authorize fallback. A classified headless refusal traverses only the configured model/harness order; a model pin stops. A bare harness pin keeps that harness and its existing model fallthrough.

Interactive refusal keeps the thread and provider visible and sends no automatic follow-up input, with or without a Task. After native authentication or capacity recovers, retry deliberately. A stopped Task is inspected with lf task status <ID> and continued with lf task run <ID>. Earlier receipts and conversation history survive.

Counterexample retained

A rejection event can arrive before the provider explains its reset time. Completing the turn on that event loses the explanation. The repair waits for the result or native exit. It adds no reset timer, quota polling, account lifecycle or extra dispatch. Existing provider-error and receipt records carry the information.

Review questions, resolved by the working design

  1. Does refusal recovery require another product concept? No. Keep the provider message on the existing error and stop-record paths.
  2. Does Task membership authorize interactive replay? No. Headless policy controls automatic continuation.
