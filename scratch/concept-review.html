Do the steering concepts line up?

Area: how a person's steer reaches a managed conversation on each of the four providers, and how a work session's launch (model, reach, idle state) matches its worker. Third pass, at dc8cf24b01, against origin/main: the agent_inbox/agent_socket split, switchModel on reopen and the two reopen QA fixes are built.

One interaction, walked

Someone runs lf steer LOO-974 'use the staging bucket' while the Task's OpenCode work session is closed. Its last turn had failed on quota, so no turn end was ever reported.

  1. The steer is stored; nothing is reachable, so it waits.
  2. The person reopens. The held terminal's status stream reaches the new provider first: begin_program_status moves session_activity to it and, new in this pass, sets observed_at=0.
  3. The plugin loads and names its socket. record_agent_inbox writes agent_socket, sees a change, and resets the row to idle because it belongs to another process or reads observed_at=0.
  4. The wake runs: IdleReach::Inbox(Inbox::Socket), {"thread","text"}, the plugin calls switchModel once (new: the wake path, not only the prompt hook), then session.synthetic. The turn answers on the model in agent:.
  5. Failure: the socket answers nothing; steers stay undelivered and ride the next boundary as context.

Normal path, failure and recovery hold. QA on a real TUI 2026-11-22 (scratch/qa.html) showed model-switched then the woken turn; the turn itself failed on quota, as every OpenCode model on the account was out.

Now

Whoever reaches session_activity first with a new agent_process_id decides what the row says. The status stream keeps the earlier process's yielded=0; the boundary resets to idle only on a process change or observed_at=0. Claude and Codex never take the reset (they name no socket on reopen, or claim steers at start). The QA bug this pass fixed was exactly this race.

PROPOSED (LOO-1068, not this Task)

One store function, called by every writer that changes agent_process_id, sets the fresh-process reading: idle, no open tools, no status, attachment from agent_sessions. The observed_at=0 clause and the reset inside record_agent_inbox go away; record_agent_inbox only saves an address and asks for a wake. With it, a reopen with steers waiting starts a turn on every provider (2a), once the reason Claude's start hook claims steers is found.

Why not here: deciding "fresh = idle" for Claude and Codex changes their reopen behavior, which is 3b, already split out by answer and filed. The sentinel is the smallest correct patch inside this Task's scope.
