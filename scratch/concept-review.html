Failure → recovery

Unknown conversation activity adds no badge. Saved startup content strips activity and run/ship permissions. An incomplete read retains last-good content, panes and drafts; fresh subscriptions restore current facts. No new store or polling loop. Mutations still recheck current state.

Keep the counterexamples: never-started completion offers Reopen; started work ignores conflicting planning completion; Available and Busy can coexist; saved Dashboard arrangements and Done dates keep their own order. NOW condition remains separate from lifecycle.

Counterexample retained: a Blocked conversation may belong to an Available Task that also matches Busy. Report views keep Blocked and its source; Rust aggregates all conversations for filters and the board. A reported Done never completes the Task. Review questions, resolved by the working design

  1. Does unassignment mean never started? No. Keep Active and change ownership/section.
  2. Does an open Session imply Busy? No. Require current activity facts.
  3. Does a retained Auto-Ship handoff imply runtime Shipping? No. Keep Stop Auto-Ship through restart or unassignment.
  4. Must a pane equal its Task's aggregate activity? No. Share wording, retain report scope and provenance.
