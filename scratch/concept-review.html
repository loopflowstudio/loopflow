Keep one operation and one receipt

A terminal or Desktop starts the same Task and retains its cursor, native thread and input. A lost launch reply keeps the creation key and receipt; retry cannot launch twice. Ending a request or watch cannot stop its detached execution. Preserve request-local directory and I/O, uncertain outcomes, once-only usage and waited landing. No new Work record or execution dispatcher.

Keep CLI/server fixtures above the cut and compile the execution owner alone. Move implementations to their owners and delete old bodies. Keep wire results shared; render them at the terminal edge. A Machine recording gap must still leave the provider conversation running.

Build and recover

scripts/dev-lf --version refreshes derived roots before building. A new root can miss until the daemon exits naturally; the next daemon reads the updated configuration. Bare Cargo does not refresh roots. No restart, shared target or new scheduler. Cache failure permits direct compilation; scripts/dev-lf --isolated-build --version supplies local debug sources.

The verified experimental cache remains a prerequisite for demonstrated reuse. Root upkeep alone does not establish a production cache installation or speedup.
