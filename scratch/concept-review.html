Draft Desktop usage copy · live acceptance pending

Submit a turn and follow its reported state in the pane and work summary. Working stays visible during quiet work. Blocked identifies a question, permission request or sign-in. A finished turn shows the provider's Idle or Done. Reconnect to recover the latest report without sending another prompt. A cleared report leaves no program-status label; it does not complete the Task.

Existing transport guidance and skill instructions need no rewrite.

What would disprove the repair?

A real provider finishes without a settlement report. Desktop stores Idle but still displays Working. Reconnect loses a pending question or restores an old claim. Any of these requires repair; elapsed time cannot stand in for missing evidence.
