Advance with the existing design

No new product choice or implementation direction. A creation can finish after you leave; its reply must respect where you went.

The mismatch and the implemented repair

Before: submit A → open Task B and a new draft → A replies → selection jumps to A and the new sheet closes.

Implemented: submit A → open Task B and a new draft → A replies → B and its draft stay. A remains recoverable until its result is presented.

Returning to the same Task or reopening New Task does not restore the earlier reply’s right to navigate or dismiss the sheet.

Normal, failure, recovery

A normal reply opens its conversation. Storage failure keeps the original form without sending. A lost reply leaves the saved request for receipt lookup; a lookup failure never becomes absence. Leaving the presentation changes only UI behavior, without canceling admitted work or losing recovery identity.

Work readings, terminal continuation and provider transitions keep separate recovery contracts. Seeing a result grants no input, geometry or provider-launch authority.
