Doctor should say when the system ended Desktop, not when its search stopped

LOO-683 · concept-review, third pass (headless) · 2026-10-29 · built from scratch/loo-683-desktop-exits.md, rust/loopflow/src/desktop_exits.rs, and a live /usr/bin/log show on Jack's Mac

Most of the model holds. The journal holds exit facts. lf.log holds diagnostics. lf doctor is the one reader. One mismatch remains, and it lands on the exit the live acceptance produces: a Force Quit, which nothing in the app can record.

What Jack does

Force Quits Loopflow, relaunches it, and runs lf doctor.

What doctor prints

Loopflow 0.15.1 (pid 9446) was killed by SIGKILL (Force Quit, kill -9, or the system) before 09:16:00, 1h18m after launch; last vitals 09:10: 412 MB, 61 lf reads/5 min

Existing behavior: the time is the end of the search window, up to 6 minutes late, and the lifetime is measured to it.

Loopflow 0.15.1 (pid 9446) was killed 09:14:02 by SIGKILL (Force Quit, kill -9, or the system), 1h12m after launch; last vitals 09:10: 412 MB, 61 lf reads/5 min

Proposed, and this is the Demo line already accepted.

Why it is available

2026-10-29 07:27:25.606 Df runningboardd[401:bc6806b] … :52901] termination reported by launchd (0, 0, 256)

Doctor already reads this line. It parses the tuple and drops the timestamp. That time is local, with no offset, so the query should use --style ndjson or --timezone UTC.
