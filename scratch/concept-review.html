The mismatch, before → proposed

Existing implementation: Desktop surface exists → “Already open,” or focus → usable. A failed attachment can retain that surface. Desktop opening also maps elsewhere to usable.

Proposed: view identity answers where; its existing owner answers ready, pending or failed. A focus result confirms focus. Only attachment confirms readiness. Derive these facts; add no persistent registry.

One command through recovery

lf open --task ENG-123 --host desktop

NormalResolve Session → attach → confirm readiness. Later terminal opening reports the existing location.
FailureAttachment fails; keep the pane and error. A surface alone cannot confirm success.
Retry · proposedCorrect the cause; repeat the command. Reuse Desktop's pane and retry through the Session owner without replaying consumed input.
CounterexampleA timeout may leave attachment running. Report pending instead of launching a competitor. If it completed, report its ready view.
