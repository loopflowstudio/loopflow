Failure and recovery

LF-owned remote routing sends heartbeats every ten seconds. Thirty seconds without them ends the remote relay even without TCP EOF. The detached execution owner stays independent. Dated owner observations also expire after an owner crash.

From detected loss, the same fifteen-minute absence window applies. Another connected viewer retains throughout. Reconnecting cancels cleanup—even a brief return between cleanup checks. No extra fifteen minutes follows expiry.

Orderly Desktop Quit sends leave and waits up to two seconds for relay exit. Failed delivery follows disconnect cleanup. An independently opened external SSH session uses its host's loss detection; LF's heartbeat bound applies to LF-owned routing.

Expiry does not prove the remote client died. After a long outage a completed Task's checkout may be gone. Unfinished Tasks are not deleted, and conversation history remains. Existing merged-delivery, committed-ref and persistent-checkout safeguards remain; PR-less checkouts retain their existing safeguard.
