The biggest mismatch: belonging and conducting are two relations

A conversation belongs to a Task: it works in the Task's checkout, files work in its name, and direction for the Task is direction for it. A conversation conducts a Task: it is told when that Task needs someone. The person never confuses these. The kickoff plan gave them one definition by adding children to session_tasks().

Before: one relation, widened

Everything keyed on membership moves with it: steer audience (steers.rs:411), conversation_task (moves.rs:165, exactly one Task or none), the context block, the steer marker, session completion, retirement's "hosted" Tasks.

After: two relations, the second derived proposed

session_tasks() is untouched. Conducted Tasks are the Tasks whose parent is one of the Session's Tasks. It is read in two places only: the update writer, and the audience of one new steer source.

Closing a parent

Kickoff plan

$ lf task complete LOO-937
LOO-956 has an open PR.
Pass --keep LOO-956 or --abandon LOO-956.

A stop, two flags, and a write that moves a kept child to another parent.

After proposed

$ lf task complete LOO-937
LOO-937 done.
  LOO-954 done (merged #1990)
  LOO-955 abandoned (never started)
  LOO-956 left open · at human-review · PR #1992

No stop, no flag, no child ever moves. A child shows under its parent while the parent is open and top-level otherwise; the same rule already covered a parent unknown to this Machine.

Counterexamples kept. Auto-Ship completes a parent at merge: it would abandon the follow-ups not yet started, so Auto-Ship's merge does not complete a Task with open children. A never-started child you still wanted: it is abandoned with the parent; abandoning an unstarted Task removes no checkout or branch. A child with unpublished commits: counts as unmerged and stays open.

Choices made for review

  1. What a conductor hears about a child.
    1. Chosen: only a change of standing (stopped for a person, failed, merged, closed).
    2. Everything the child's own conversation hears, step by step.
    3. Standing changes, plus a digest of steps when the conductor is next woken for another reason.
  2. lf task complete on a parent with a child that has unmerged work.
    1. Chosen: close the parent, leave that child open with its link, name it.
    2. Stop and ask (--keep / --abandon).
    3. Leave the parent open until every child is closed.
  3. A never-started child when its parent closes.
    1. Chosen: abandoned.
    2. Done, as Linear's "mark remaining sub-issues done" setting does.
    3. Left in the backlog, top-level.
  4. A closed parent's conversation and a child that is still open.
    1. Chosen: it still hears, whenever it is open.
    2. Hearing ends when the parent closes.
  5. A child's Project.
    1. Chosen: copied from the parent at filing; it does not follow the parent later.
    2. Found by climbing to the parent each time it is read.
  6. A Task filed by a Graph step of LOO-937 (launch-plan, ship-decomposed), not by its conversation.
    1. Chosen: a child of LOO-937, like any other.
    2. Top-level unless --parent is passed.
