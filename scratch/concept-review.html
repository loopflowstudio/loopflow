One afternoon, as built now

Two Claude Max accounts. jack@ is active at 60% of its weekly window, which resets in 5 days: 40% left over 120 hours scores 333. work@ is at 30%, resetting in 3 hours: 70% over 3 hours scores 23 333.

  • 14:00 A Graph step launches. route_rank puts work@ first; the Machine's Claude login switches and every open Claude follows at its next message.
  • 14:05 You run lf account claude use jack@, wanting to stay on it.
  • 14:06 The next launch ranks work@ ahead again and switches back. Nothing told you use would not stick.
  • 17:00 work@ resets having been used first. That was the point of the decision, and it works.
