The consequential distinction is now explicit.

Before → reviewed branch

Before: authored settings could become CLI authority in exported commands, and conversation selection could become future Task policy.

Implemented on this branch: captures retain occurrence authority; recipes resolve current preferences in the launch repository. Session history remains execution history. Explicit Graph selection reaches composed steps and routers; an agent's unrelated child commands resolve their own settings.

The old Task agent field is removed. No replacement policy record, resolution snapshot lifecycle, or settings synchronization is required for this revision.

Failure and recovery

An invalid preference is corrected or unset before launch. Unset reveals lower layers; false replaces a boolean; [] clears earlier list contributions. Those actions must stay distinct.

Native resume preserves its provider and recorded account. The recorded fallback assumption permits a same-provider model refresh, without requesting cross-provider import. A model limit during work still has no automatic recovery in PR1.

PR2 requirement, unimplemented: interactive Sessions and headless steps continue on eligible accounts/models, or wait truthfully for reset and resume. The mechanism remains undesigned here; original thread/account attribution and work must survive. Catalogue and portability designs also remain separate proposals.

Resolved review questions

  1. Change the precedence rule? No. A high-source fallback losing to a lower-source choice is required behavior. Preserve this counterexample in gate.
  2. Add policy to Task or Session history? No. Conversation selection must not select later Task workers. Explicit Graph invocation supplies its own intent.
  3. Any open product choice for PR1? None found. Next proof remains the affected gate suites and demo, including inspection/export parity, repeated composition, native continuity and false/empty/unset behavior.
