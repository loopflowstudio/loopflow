The mismatch: the heartbeat is read but never shown

Doctor finds the last vitals line for the pid, then uses only its timestamp. The heartbeat exists so a storm or a memory climb shows up beside the exit. On 2026-10-29 it would have put about 1,000 killed reads next to the exit(1). An unrecorded end also returns before its lifetime is added.

Implemented

⚠ desktop-exit  Loopflow 0.15.1 (pid 9446) was killed by SIGKILL (Force Quit, kill -9, or the system) before 09:16

Proposed (the accepted Demo)

⚠ desktop-exit  Loopflow 0.15.1 (pid 9446) was killed by SIGKILL (Force Quit, kill -9, or the system) before 09:16, 1h12m after launch; last vitals 09:10: 412 MB, 61 lf reads/5 min

Rule: every ended line carries its lifetime. Each abnormal end appends its last vitals (footprint, reads, and the killed count when it's nonzero), or nothing when no vitals line exists. The storm WARN line counts as a vitals line. Quits stay terse.
