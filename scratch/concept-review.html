The mismatch, before → now

Before the prior passes: a rejected turn that needed manual input said “Waiting for native conversation”. Sending that input produced “Review conversation”, which stayed; even “Continued” never ended.

Implemented now: each row says what to do. Manual continuation ends the row instead of creating another warning.

The row ends at your next message. Input queued behind a running resend starts afterward. Unconfirmed delivery keeps its warning until native history distinguishes the resend from your later input.

Normal, failure, recovery

Normal: Waiting → Recovering → Continued → your next message → no row. The selected model, history, draft and pending approval stay with the native client.

Failure: a lost installation or resend reply remains uncertain. A second click or server reconnect cannot authorize another dispatch.

Recovery: your later message ends the old row. If that message hits a limit too, its own failed turn gets a new row. A late old-worker result cannot replace it.
