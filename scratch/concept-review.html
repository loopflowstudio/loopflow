The mismatch to avoid

“Direct a child” must not promise a live conversation with a plain headless run. Its brief is its direction. A finished run cannot be resumed; carry its result into another child's brief. The work guidance now says this.

Failure and recovery

If the server is unavailable, the hook uses its last saved identity and records a gap. A later successful managed start or compaction refreshes it. Bare runs keep their launch snapshot. Multiple Task associations omit the Task line; large files cannot consume identity's reserved context space.
