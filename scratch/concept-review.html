4. Choices made for review & scope boundaries

  • choice made Unpausing at Paused: end stays open for explicit ✓ Complete (Option B): Clicking ▶ (or running lf task unpause [--run]) lifts the hold and shows Done with accented ✓ rather than auto-completing and vanishing the Task from Working. Passed over requesting the withheld auto-completion on unpause (Option A) because ▶ should never silently close a Task, and ✓ is already clickable from Paused: end.
  • choice made Preserve top-level step in every non-running C1 chip label: Paused: build · implement, Pausing: deliver · land --wait-and-fix, Waiting: deliver · land --wait-and-fix; narrow panes tail-truncate the chip while hover keeps the full sentence.
  • Outside LOO-1078 (separate outcomes): (1) Holistic session-vs-worker availability semantics (paused sidebar rows showing Available · inferred from the open primary conversation; kept by product decision for a dedicated pass); (2) Separate-session Codex 0.171.0 tool jobs (osPid: null in app-server; named gap for PR); (3) Pause issued in the narrow window after a move payload is sent and before the destination checkout exists (named gap for PR); (4) Pre-existing task_complete leaving a running Graph step alive until it exits, and pre-existing raw step UUID in task_execution.rs:336 (#1974).
