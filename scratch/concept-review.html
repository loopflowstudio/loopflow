Opening work, with every view editable

LOO-935 at 2ef889c396, against main (470da475). This replaces the review of 6e82d34aba. Nothing here changes code.

Area taken: how a person reaches a conversation and works in it from more than one place: lf open, lf resume, overviews, Desktop panes, the phone, and terminal size. Shared input changed what every one of these means, so all of them are reviewed together. The store reduction is left out; it has no concept a person sees.

One interaction, start to finish

You're at your Mac, LOO-935's conversation is open in Desktop, and you go to the couch with your laptop.

laptop: lf resume LOO-935→ one more view; you type, so the laptop sets the size; Desktop shows the same draft→ help still says "a terminal showing it hands it over and says where it went"→ close the laptop; size goes back to Desktop

Then the same morning from the phone:

phone opens LOO-935 and fits the terminal to the phone→ you touch the Mac again, and the Mac takes the size→ phone stops drawing: "This Session is open on another device." · Continue here

Where it breaks: the laptop and Desktop act as two windows onto one shared terminal, but the phone acts as if one device holds it. It's the same event, another device taking the size, and the two clients respond with different models. And a person who knows only "I can type from anywhere" is told "open on another device", which is the old model's words.

The recovery works: pressing Continue here takes the size back and nothing is sent twice.

The biggest mismatch: lf resume and lf open are now one action

Before this PR, the two verbs differed in one real way: lf open never took input, and lf resume moved it to you. Shared input removed that difference, so they now do the same thing. A person has to learn two commands for one action, and the docs teach both in the same sentence.

Now

lf resume                  # this checkout's conversation, here
lf resume LOO-12           # a Task's, here
lf open --task LOO-12      # the same, here
lf open --task LOO-12 --tab
lf resume <id> --fork      # a new conversation from it
lf resume -a codex         # continue on Codex

Proposed

lf open                    # this checkout's conversation, here
lf open LOO-12             # a Task's (URL, ID or Session)
lf open LOO-12 --tab | --focus | --workspace
lf session fork <id>       # a new conversation from it
lf session move <id> -a codex   # continue on another harness
# lf resume: deleted

lf open keeps the name because it's the one with the destinations (--tab, --focus, --host, --workspace, --available). What lf resume did beyond opening is to make a new conversation: --fork, and -a to switch harness. Those belong with lf session move, which already does "continue Claude Sessions on Codex".

What breaks it: lf resume is named in the lf context block every agent gets ("Never lf resume your own Session"), in builtin skills, in muscle memory, and in the launch table in transport/README.md. The rename touches all of those in one diff, with no alias, per the repo rule. The opposite option is to keep lf resume as the verb and make lf open the command for hosts and overviews. That leaves an overlap at --here, which is exactly where the two are the same today.

This is larger than LOO-935 either way: it's a rename across the product. The PR's own part is smaller and is a bug: the stale "hands it over" text in lf resume's help and in resume_explanation.rs should say what's built.

Smaller findings

Proposed The phone joins the shared model. When another device takes the size, the phone keeps drawing at the other device's size, scaled to fit or scrollable, and keeps its Reply box. Touching or typing takes the size back, the same as everywhere else. State.elsewhere and Continue here go away. The counterexample: a 120-column screen drawn on a phone is unreadable. If that's the real reason the phone blanks, the honest label is "Sized for your Mac · Fit to phone", not "open on another device".

Proposed Rename --no-focus. --background keeps the host's current tab selected. That frees "focus" for revealing a view, which is now also what gives a view the size.

Unchanged: --local-workspace could become an internal command, and lf desktop layout could merge into lf desktop dashboard slots. Both are small, and both were open in the last review.
