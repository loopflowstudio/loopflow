Earlier review: a delayed wait could hide dispatch; reading history before sending left newer input exposed. Built: retained facts keep one attempt across reconnect, and the native submission hook holds admission through credential preparation. Only its correlated completion establishes “Continued”.

Current usage: “Checking again does not resend a dispatched retry. Its progress follows the original attempt, even after a reconnect.” See Session usage. No new usage or skill workflow is proposed.

Normal, failure, recovery

Two limited Task conversations continue on eligible logins, retaining their models, history and drafts. No capacity: keep waiting and show a known reset. A client without the guard stays open with an explanation. Cancelled or unconfirmed dispatch: keep the attempt and show “Review conversation”; a late correlated completion can settle it. An already-sent login change may take effect after cancellation without releasing the retry.

Remaining mismatch: served native fixtures establish continuation, drafts and selected approval/cancellation cases. They do not establish the rendered Desktop experience, physical typing after reopen, or installed real-login behavior. Neither account adoption nor an unrelated completed turn closes that gap.
