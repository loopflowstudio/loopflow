No decision is needed from you to continue. This page records one consequence of your November 4 contract, which the branch had blurred, so implementation can follow it. The questions at the end are for the demo, not for this step.

Two questions, one piece of evidence

Each Rex Conversation pane now carries an observer record with an OS lock. It answers is this conversation live right now, and lf open reports it. The plan then said retention still needed “a reliable terminal-to-Session association” and that native IDs “cannot stand in for live holds, including after restart.” Read together, that pushes toward using observer liveness, or something new, as the cleanup hold.

Your contract says something different. A retained terminal holds the Task regardless of focus. Only confirmed removal releases it. Unknown inventory never does. A tab Rex keeps after its observer exits is still a view you kept.

What changes in the next implement pass

  1. One Rex inventory read per Task produces held · released · unknown from its occurrences, including named copies and Wave conversation containers. It goes to the shared lifetime owner. No adapter timer, closure or settlement.
  2. Observer records stay readiness-only.
  3. failed for an observer that simply stopped becomes ended. Only the lock's absence is known. failed keeps meaning an attachment error was reported.

Nothing new is needed for terminal-to-Session association: HostOccurrence already records Task → destination. Native IDs still never authorize replay or prove the original programs survived. Those are separate questions.

Counterexample kept open

You restore a Rex session, forget a completed Task's tab, and its checkout stays until you close that tab. That follows from “retaining a view is the reason to keep.” If the demo shows you don't want it, it's a new product choice. It should not be handled by reintroducing away detection.
