Failure and recovery

A failed acquisition or preflight preserves the prior installation. After store advancement, retry finishes the receipt's recorded destination before considering a new request. Matching files still pass through promotion to reconcile the receipt and gate. Desktop alone is quiesced and reopened; live retired bundles remain available to their owners.

The contract fails if a schedule loses the custom location, CLI-only leaves an existing app behind, recovery follows a new environment instead of its receipt, or cleanup removes a still-needed image. Focused source fixtures cover these boundaries; the activation fixture does not prove the full production-store recovery transaction.
