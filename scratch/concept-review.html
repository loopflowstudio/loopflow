The important fit with 717

717 currently releases every Desktop Task on departure. This design keeps recent unfinished Tasks retained instead. We change that decision in the same Desktop owner.

Eviction or Close releases the view and hold. Active work, drafts and pending requests remain protected. Completion removes background warmth: eligible agents end after two minutes and shells/checkouts retire at fifteen minutes total under 717.

Eight recent conversations is a provisional starting value. Visible panes are never evicted automatically. Other hosts and protected work can retain additional agents; eight is not a machine-wide process cap.
