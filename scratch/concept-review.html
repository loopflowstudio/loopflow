A conversation can move a Task

Existing. The Machine sorts unsaved rows by whether a Session is Available or Blocked, then by rank. Typing can move B and change its keyboard slot without any change to its Task record. Saved drag positions happen to mask the problem.

Proposed. One shared order uses saved positions, then rank and canonical Task ID. Session reports update status, attention and activity filter membership. A user can choose the Available filter for a queue that changes with who needs attention.

What survives a refresh

Busy, then Available again
B stays second in Working and keeps its conversation. It disappears from Available during the turn and appears in Busy. If a second Session is waiting, B can appear in both.
The read fails
Keep the last confirmed rows where a reading exists. A successful empty read clears them. Unknown activity can make an activity filter incomplete.
A dragged target leaves Working
The Machine can refuse the drop. Clear the preview even if the refresh fails. When the read returns, reflect current membership and order. A later drag against a current target can succeed.
The saved order arrives before the write answer
Keep the preview on the chosen side of its target. Moving into the target’s index again could flip the pair back. Retire the preview when a read covers the write.
A Task joins after the first drag
It joins the rows after the saved positions unless it has a saved place. Rank and Task ID order that tail.
A query or dashboard has its own arrangement
Built-in scopes and simple filters take their sequence from the Machine. Authored query clauses keep their composition order. Touched Dashboards use their saved arrangement.
