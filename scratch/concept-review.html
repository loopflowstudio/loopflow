4. Choices made for review & scope boundaries

  • choice made Check natural stops before interrupted() in graph.rs: When a draining operation exits 0 and the next step is already a stop (stands_in == None or stopped_landing == Some), the Graph genuinely arrived at that stop rather than being interrupted mid-run. Passed over keeping interrupted() first because it invented a phantom Run state the person never expects.
  • choice made Keep paused Tasks open when a draining final command reaches end: Skip request_completion_in in arrive_in while a task_pauses row exists. Passed over auto-completing because a paused Task must never close itself or erase its hold without the person’s click.
  • choice made Keep the saved cursor while paused, settle dead stop cursors on unpause: Letting Auto toggle off and back on while paused keeps Resume reversible until unpause; once unpaused with Auto off, release settles a dead interactive/landing cursor to At { step } in the same transaction.
  • Outside LOO-1078 (separate outcomes): (1) Holistic session-vs-worker availability semantics (paused sidebar rows showing Available · inferred from the open primary conversation); (2) Separate-session Codex 0.171.0 tool jobs (osPid: null in app-server); (3) Pause issued in the narrow window after a move payload is sent and before the destination checkout exists; (4) Pre-existing task_complete leaving a running Graph step alive until it exits, and pre-existing raw step UUID in task_execution.rs:336 (#1974).
