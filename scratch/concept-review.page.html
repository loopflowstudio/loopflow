Actionable mismatch: "This applies to every decider" in DECISION_CRITERIA contradicts optimize-plan

Pass 2 (41bce8d68a) restored the non-convergence stop for realign and implement by moving the blocked bullet above the skill-override sentence and marking it mandatory for every decider. Walk a normal optimize run to see the counterexample:

  1. Pass 1: Hypothesis A cuts latency 15% and is kept. optimize-plan returns iterate.
  2. Pass 2: Hypothesis B regresses latency and is reverted. The branch now has zero new progress from Pass 2, and either Hypothesis C is ready or no worthwhile ideas remain.
  3. optimize-plan decides: Its skill prompt says to iterate on the next hypothesis, advance when no clear way forward remains (so compress → refresh → measure keep Pass 1's 15% win), and return blocked only when the harness or workload has failed.
  4. What the runner appends at the end of the prompt: DECISION_CRITERIA now tells the model that blocked on "the last pass made no progress" or "passes repeat without converging" "applies to every decider", and restricts skill overrides to advance and iterate only — instructing optimize-plan to abort the Graph as blocked instead of testing the next idea or advancing to compress and final measurement.
Elevates "the last pass made no progress" to every decider unconditionally. Restricts skill rules to advance and iterate, overriding optimize-plan's narrower blocked rule. In optimize, running out of worthwhile ideas finishes with advance so compress, refresh, and final measurement run. Direct contradiction with output.rs:14 ("This applies to every decider"). Documents the same false universal right above the optimize section (line 226).

Before (HEAD)

DECISION_CRITERIA says blocked on non-convergence or "the last pass made no progress" "applies to every decider", and lets skills override only advance and iterate.

Failure mode: Directly contradicts optimize-plan.md:24-28 at the end of its prompt, risking a false blocked after a reverted experiment instead of trying the next hypothesis or advancing to compress and final measurement.

After (shared default stop that respects a skill's own stop rule)

DECISION_CRITERIA keeps the non-convergence / no-progress / repeated-failure / missing-input blocked rule ahead of the advance/iterate defaults, qualified by "unless the skill says to handle that case differently" (and mirrored in docs/orchestration.md:190-195).

Result: loop-or-next, implement, and realign keep the non-convergence circuit breaker (none of them opt out), while optimize-plan keeps advancing cleanly when out of hypotheses after reverted trials.
