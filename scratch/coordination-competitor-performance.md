## Ranked additions

### 1. Count the entire idle observer loop

**Existing competitor test.** T3's [startup catch-up test](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/relay/AgentAwarenessRelay.test.ts#L842) advances ten fake minutes after success and requires no additional activity-snapshot or secret reads. Its [tool-output test](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/relay/AgentAwarenessRelay.test.ts#L677) requires no shell reads for transcript/ongoing-tool updates, then delivers completion when background work ends. Paseo's [degraded polling test](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.observation.test.ts#L1592) checks 5/10/20/40/60-second intervals, six polls over 195 seconds instead of 39, and reset after a change. Its [implementation](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.ts#L1825) schedules the next timer after completion.

**LF coverage/gap.** `harness/task_updates.rs:396` measures `TaskUpdates::pending` across history growth, 1/16 observers and unrelated writes. The enclosing native `watch` also checks process evidence and enters provider context every second (`:267`); Codex's timer also checks attachment/selection (`codex_connection.rs:533`). Those costs are outside that measurement. `tests/performance_tests.rs:86` separately covers CLI wait store reuse. Neither establishes installed total CPU.

**Proposed invariant.** Baseline, then exercise real observer-loop iterations with a quiet store and unrelated active Sessions. Require zero Git/provider children, zero history rebuilds, zero fresh store/runtime opens after warm-up, and bounded total SQL/rows/VM work per observer tick independent of history. Count wakeups separately from queries; dropped observers must stop generating both. Establish the full-loop numeric query budget from a corrected run, not by reusing `pending`'s one-query budget. Do not copy T3's zero-poll rule or Paseo's 60-second cap: LF still needs unrecorded-exit detection, delivery retries and settings freshness.

### 2. Prove cancellation removes work, including queued initialization

**Existing competitor tests.** Paradev's [cancellation tests](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/workspace-fs/src/watch-cancellation.test.ts#L33) share one initialization between two streams, cancel only after the second leaves, occupy four scan slots, cancel a fifth queued scan, and prove it never ran while a fresh subscription succeeds. The [queue implementation](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/workspace-fs/src/watch.ts#L735) removes canceled waiters and checks cancellation before scanning. Its [no-subscriber test](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/chat-runtime/src/stream/subscriptions/subscriptionResilience.test.ts#L90) asserts orphan deltas schedule zero callbacks; [last removal](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/chat-runtime/src/stream/subscriptions/subscriptions.ts#L193) disposes its coalescer. Paseo [closes an observer abandoned during asynchronous setup](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.observation.test.ts#L406).

**LF coverage/gap.** `server/service.rs:1226` checks cancellation, no later checkpoint acceptance and sibling progress. `tests/session_list_subscribe.rs:77` checks EOF/closed stdout retires the reader while leaving the provider alive. `tests/linear/subscribe.rs:470` checks parent exit despite an inherited open pipe. `lf/commands/subscribe.rs:304,826` closes mailboxes and removes the store observer after its last follower. These cover behavior, but the inspected tests do not count residual queued reads, wake threads or reads completing after canceled setup.

**Proposed invariant.** Stall initialization and a read independently; cancel one of two subscribers, then the last. The surviving reader still works; canceled queued work never starts; after admitted blocking work settles, observer/follower/queued-read counts return to baseline and later clock ticks do no work. Reopen repeatedly and require no accumulation. Scope this to observers: accepted mutations and provider lifetimes must survive viewer cancellation.

### 3. Bound duplicate observation and refresh bursts

**Existing competitor tests.** Paseo's [second-client test](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.observation.test.ts#L368) requires exactly one facts read, one status read, one checkout watcher and one Git-directory watcher for two clients. Its [ten-worktree test](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.observation.test.ts#L1464) requires ten checkout targets but one repository metadata/fetch target, and retains fetch after the first subscriber leaves. T3's [100-enqueue test](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/relay/AgentAwarenessRelay.test.ts#L307) produces exactly three publications: initial A, latest B, latest A. A [set of queued thread IDs](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/relay/AgentAwarenessRelay.ts#L139) permits one trailing refresh during an in-flight publication.

**LF coverage/gap.** LF already shares one store observer per database *per process* (`lf/commands/subscribe.rs:800`), uses a changed-checkout set and a three-second checkout rest (`subscribe/checkouts.rs:12`), and coalesces reads with 100 ms quiet/250 ms maximum delay (`subscribe.rs:45,885`). `tests/linear/subscribe.rs:404` checks a steady writer remains visible; `:704,737` bound Git reads per repository/live checkout. Current Task-update tests cover receipt deduplication and independent run settlement. Receipt uniqueness does not count duplicate workers; the shared store observer does not share every subscription's reader/runtime or native-process observer.

**Proposed invariant.** For 1/16 in-process clients on one database, exactly one store observer remains; second clients add no duplicate checkout discovery where sharing is intended. Hold a refresh, issue 100 updates for A and B, and require at most one queued refresh per affected key, latest-state delivery and no starvation of B. Repeated hooks for the same native thread should leave at most one Task-update worker. Distinct conversations still need their own delivery and receipts. Measure before introducing cross-process sharing.

### 4. Charge slow-consumer buffers until actual release

**Existing competitor test.** T3's [LiveStreamBudget tests](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/LiveStreamBudget.test.ts#L18) retain a delivered but unacknowledged item against item/byte limits, close the source on overflow, and release all charges on scope exit. The [replay test](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/LiveStreamBudget.test.ts#L95) resumes the undelivered tail without loss/duplication. Its [filter test](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/LiveStreamBudget.test.ts#L216) excludes unrelated threads before retaining them.

**LF coverage/gap.** `tests/transport_tests.rs:1373,5592` already test slow observers and a stalled 16 MiB viewer while the owner drains. `server/service.rs:443,472` bounds message/checkpoint counts. Those are not byte budgets for arbitrarily large complete readings, and the inspected tests do not account for values retained by blocked sends. This is broader server follow-up, not a Task-update-diff defect.

**Proposed invariant.** Under a stalled reader, retained encoded bytes/items plateau at declared limits, including the in-flight send; cancellation returns usage to baseline; healthy peers/provider output continue. Resume must deliver the current complete reading or documented recovery. Prefer LF's existing latest-reading model; do not import an event-log protocol merely to copy the test.

### 5. Extend churn checks to observer resources

**Existing competitor tests.** Paradev [runs 128 real PTY natural exits](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/pty-daemon/test/fd-lifecycle.test.ts#L39), requires each master FD closed and macOS process-wide `/dev/ptmx` count back at baseline, and double-disposes safely. Paseo's [delivery memory test](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/session/owned-subscriptions/index.test.ts#L6) compares fresh processes with zero/40 closed-delivery cycles, requiring retained allocation of 4,096 subsequent promises below four times control. This detects async-context retention; it is not an idle CPU benchmark.

**LF coverage/gap.** `tests/transport/ssh_shells.rs:442` already exercises 20 reconnect/exit cycles with actual process, PTY and socket inventories. Avoid duplicating that suite. Add observer/subscription thread, FD and timer accounting to repeated attach/cancel and native-hook cycles instead.

**Proposed invariant.** After 32/128 isolated cycles and cleanup, zero net observer-owned threads, FDs, socket leases or scheduled work; next legitimate subscription still operates. Prefer exact owned-resource counts to tight RSS/wall-time thresholds. Keep live provider/terminal owners out of the reclamation budget.

## Architecture follow-up: sharing computation, not only notifications

Jack Heart: “ok, lets think big and figure out how to radically cheapen this.” Additional source inspection only; the optimize baseline owns measurements. None of these codebases establishes a literal singleton across every process on a machine. Their useful sharing lives inside a long-lived host/service instance.

**Paseo shares complete checkout computation.** [Bootstrap creates one WorkspaceGitService](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/bootstrap.ts#L954). Its [target maps](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.ts#L604) separate checkout snapshots, recursive working-tree watches and repository metadata/fetch. [Registration](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.ts#L675) adds a listener to an existing cwd target; [snapshot refresh](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.ts#L3257) joins one in-flight promise and merges additional requirements into one trailing refresh. It [computes/fingerprints once before fan-out](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.ts#L3735). [Last-listener cleanup](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/workspace-git-service.ts#L3881) removes the checkout; repository timers survive only while another workspace needs them. Cleanup clears debounce, recovery, fallback and forge polling timers. The second-client/ten-worktree tests above exercise real computation sharing, not merely shared notifications.

**Paradev shares setup and live transformation; bootstrap replay remains individual.** One [WorkspaceFilesystemManager](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/host-service/src/runtime/filesystem/filesystem.ts#L33) owns a watcher manager and service cache. [FsWatcherManager.subscribe](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/workspace-fs/src/watch.ts#L339) keys pending setup and established watchers by normalized requested absolute path, joins initialization, and counts listeners. This is not universal physical-path deduplication: symlink resolution happens later. Last release aborts pending setup or [disposes all watcher timers and its throttler](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/workspace-fs/src/watch.ts#L415). Its chat runtime [opens one database for journal, session store and SubscriptionHub](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/chat-runtime/src/index.ts#L56). The hub has [one delta coalescer per session but per-subscriber replay/filtering](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/chat-runtime/src/stream/subscriptions/subscriptions.ts#L76); zero listeners means no delta timer. [Reconnect/unsubscribe tests](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/chat-runtime/src/stream/subscriptions/subscriptions.test.ts#L188) require convergence and sibling survival. These tests do not assert identical reads are shared.

**T3 shares services and provider runtimes, not every projection.** Its [event-store service](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/persistence/OrchestrationEventStore.ts#L292) holds SQL and committed-event publication once; [each stream](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/persistence/OrchestrationEventStore.ts#L729) retains its own cursor, replay, filtering and budget. [Shell snapshot reads remain subscriber-specific](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/ShellStream.ts#L293), despite sharing one SQLite connection; decoding deliberately happens after its read transaction. Separately, [provider open takes a per-session lock and returns the existing runtime](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/ProviderSessionManager.ts#L2141). The [duplicate-open test](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/ProviderSessionManager.test.ts#L1091) requires one adapter open and object-identical results. [Idle cleanup](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code/blob/71c2d3a302092b94511eb7e6ab4e6844a66dbf22/apps/server/src/orchestration-v2/ProviderSessionManager.ts#L1125) uses generation/runtime identity and rechecks busy work after yielding. That manages provider lifetimes; it is not a safe policy to transplant into LF's passive observers.

### Three separable mechanisms to measure

1. **Reuse server execution resources before merging subscriptions.** LF `Reader::new` currently creates a Tokio runtime for each reader (`lf/commands/subscribe.rs:497`); each reader also retains its store/config observation. Compare 1/16/64 server subscriptions with one service-owned runtime and controlled read-store lifetime, keeping each reader's arguments, checkpoints and errors independent. Measure threads, store opens, idle wakes and unrelated-request latency. Paradev's single database/runtime construction and T3's shared SQL services supply a concrete ownership model; they do **not** test the effect of Rust runtime pooling. A shared connection must not let a slow projection monopolize other reads or mix snapshots. This can be a small resource-ownership change without a new persistent broker.

2. **Compute one reading per identical demand, fan out separate delivery envelopes.** Paseo supplies the strongest implementation and tests: one target, one in-flight read, one latest value, one trailing invalidation. First measure identical LF reads separately from distinct Task reads; runtime reuse alone may already remove most cost. If duplicate projections dominate, key sharing by database, operation and full arguments/context; keep `--select`, delivery state and checkpoint acknowledgments per consumer where they affect only formatting. An explicit checkpoint must observe a snapshot fresh enough to answer that request, even if the body is unchanged; joining a pre-checkpoint in-flight result without another read can violate that. Paseo's force/join mechanism is inspiration, not proof of LF's checkpoint contract. Cancellation removes demand, never another consumer's delivery identity. Paseo's [independent-release test](https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo/blob/71439e51a555bfca21dc21e74bcfb39f1eb5e760/packages/server/src/server/owned-subscriptions.e2e.test.ts#L676) demonstrates the distinction.

3. **Schedule changed keys and necessary deadlines, rather than every consumer's periodic work.** T3's queued-ID worker performs one latest-state publication plus one trailing rerun; Paradev's last listener removes the timer entirely. Measure whether LF can reduce many per-reader revision/config checks to one database/config observation followed by affected-key invalidations. Shared notification alone is insufficient: LF already has that within a process. Retain bounded fallback for missed filesystem events, settings edits, failed reads, busy delivery and unrecorded child exit; a live graph is different demand from a settled Task. Measure actual wakes and completed reads under burst/churn/idle, not just successful delivery. After final observer release, scheduled observer work should reach zero while any admitted mutation finishes and the provider keeps running.

**Keep the two lifetimes separate.** Server read subscriptions may share runtime, store access and computation. Native Codex/Claude/Pi observers are attached to existing conversations and execution owners; pooling their LF-side observation must not require those owners to adopt a new server or die with it. Paseo and Paradev also have [host-owned live-session registries](https://github.com/ParadevAI/Paradev/blob/61645f4852d3f7ecca7f58a6f6d68d7bc9dc460c/packages/chat-runtime/src/sessions/registry/registry.ts#L77); that ownership model differs from LF. Keep native attachment/generation identity, receipt deduplication, unrecorded-exit detection and reconnect history recovery even if observation becomes shared. No measured result yet selects among these mechanisms or justifies a machine-wide orchestration rewrite.
