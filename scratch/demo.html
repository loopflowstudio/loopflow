Parent Task Completion — Prior Art & Sheet Variants

LOO-1094 · Comparing how Linear, Asana, Jira, and macOS handle open children vs. Loopflow Desktop

1. How other products treat completing a parent with open children

Linear

No modal · optional team setting

Marking a parent Done leaves open sub-issues untouched by default (they stay open under the done parent). Team workflow settings offer optional Sub-issue auto-close, which marks remaining sub-issues Done — never cancels/abandons them or deletes work. Destructive menu items stay neutral text (tint on hover).

Asana

No modal · fully independent

Parent and subtasks are independent by design. Checking off the parent completes it immediately with no warning dialog; incomplete subtasks remain open underneath it unless a custom paid Rule overrides that.

Jira (Close Sprint / Parent)

Modal · destination picker

Shows a compact modal listing open items with a choice of where they go (Backlog, Next sprint, or parent), then plain Cancel and one primary Complete button. No red destructive button in the complete flow.

macOS HIG ("Save / Don't Save / Cancel")

At most one emphasized button

Even though Don't Save discards work, it is neutral grey, not red. Save is the single accent button; Don't Save and Cancel are neutral. Red is reserved for single-purpose delete confirms (Delete / Cancel).
