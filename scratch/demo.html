Before and after

Before (main, the incident): lf open ran open -b com.loopflow.mac, and Launch Services picked any registered copy carrying that id, on 2026-11-02 a benchmark staging build.

After (this branch): one Desktop destination. The installed app by path, found through ~/.local/bin/lf, which on this Mac resolves into /Applications/Loopflow.app. A bundle id names only the non-release Desktop the caller runs inside. Release packaging alone stamps com.loopflow.mac; Xcode and lf install local builds are com.loopflow.mac.dev, and the local build stages under app.noindex/ where Spotlight does not look.
