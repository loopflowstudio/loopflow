Hero

  • "OR THE CLI", "YOUR AGENTS", "LOOPFLOW FOR MAC", "COMING SOON" were tracked mono capitals. They are sentence case in Inter.
  • Download button and install command were 44 and 54 tall. Both are 44, on one centre line.
  • The two window headers ("etude") were different heights, so their rules did not line up across the gap. They share one height.
  • The "needs you" rows lost the wine stripe down the side; they keep the wine border and the wine status word.
  • The green "running" dots no longer pulse.
  • Initials in the avatars were 9px mono; they are 12px Inter in a slightly larger circle.
  • Small text inside the windows was 10 to 11.5px in four sizes. It is 12px.
  • Left as it was: the rotating word, the two drawn windows and their "Coming soon" stamps and stripe wash. DESIGN.md argues against all three, but removing them changes what the page is, not how tidy it is.

Waves and tasks

  • "START" and "END" in the workflow lines are lower case at the same size as the steps between them.
  • The two captions under the card and the terminal were inset 4px from the boxes above them. They share the boxes' left edge.
  • Body greys (#333, #444, #3a3532) are one text color.
  • Card and terminal corners were 10 and 8. Both are 8.

The CLI

  • The Sessions and Runs terminals started at different heights because the paragraphs above them differ by a line. They start on one line.
  • The link rows ("Tasks and Waves", "The CLI") were 12px mono. They are Inter.
  • Terminal text was 12, 12.5 and 13.5px in different blocks. It is 12 or 13.

Works with and the close

  • "WORKS WITH" is sentence case.
  • The five descriptions floated at different distances under their names. Each sits directly under its name.
  • The gap before "Scale together." was two paddings stacked (about 170px). It is one section gap.
