Someone in an interactive conversation with an agent can lose their terminal, laptop or connection and reattach to the same live conversation.

Acceptance

* A detached `lf` per conversation owns the terminal and passes bytes through unchanged; loopflow does no terminal emulation of its own.
* `lf session attach <id>` works locally and over ssh, restores the modes the program set, and asks it to repaint.
* The relay is not started where `lf` already runs inside a multiplexer such as herdr or Rex, and there is no pluggable holder interface.
* The tmux launcher with its hidden command and the unreachable remote branch of human_open_argv are deleted.
* Codex keeps using its own engine socket to reattach.
* `lf` and Flows report their own program status (OSC 7501) through the same ownership, so a Flow typed into a herdr, cmux or Ghostty pane shows its state there. Jack Heart deferred this from LOO-398 on 2026-10-07 on one condition: "if lf and flows will still report to herdr just as well after that." That only holds if the Flow's own `lf` process uses this relay to hold its steps' output, so one process owns everything written to the terminal; a relay used only for interactive conversations would not deliver it. LOO-398's worker found why an owner is needed: a 74-byte report was accepted 62 bytes at a time, and step processes write to the same terminal independently.
* Writing rules carried over from LOO-398: report working, and blocked only for an explicit unanswered question, permission or sign-in, never the 120-second quiet rule; interactive hand-back is idle; headless success or failure is done or error. A provider's own reports pass through once, unchanged. `lf` writes only under its own ids (`lf/<process>` or `lf/<flow>/<step>`), clears the previous step when the Flow advances, and gives progress only for a linear Flow. No report in JSON, captured output, logs or work with no terminal.

This is step 6 of [Work on another machine · LOO-394](<https://linear.app/loopflow/issue/LOO-394>). Jack Heart decided on 2026-10-07 that each step is its own Task with its own PR, stacked on the step it builds on, so no step waits for another to land. The design is `scratch/work-on-another-machine-name.md` in this Task's worktree; this Task builds the step marked `This slice` there and nothing else.

Constraints decided by Jack Heart

* Do not lift code, tests, help text or config from herdr or cmux.
* A redesign deletes what it replaces, in the same diff.
* No stable cross-version interfaces and no support for older versions yet.
* No shared resident process; no automatic retry of a turn or Flow; no hidden arguments.
* Stop at a published PR for review in Jack's conversation; do not land.
