Three ways to
read a change.

Which experience makes you want to keep reviewing? Try the same change in three different worlds. The comparison is always the PR you would open now.

01   Precision02   Review stream03   Change stories

Proposed behavior, interactive fixtures. Code changes, live edits, conversations and PR publication are simulated. Nothing is sent or published. Built from the LOO-515 design and research, TaskFilesView.swift, TaskFileDocument.swift, and your five review priorities. No network required.

01 / File as the unit of attention

Precision

The calm of a dedicated diff app, right beside your conversation.

The appeal: see both versions clearly, one file at a time.
The cost: careful alignment and move matching; more room for the wider view. The whole PR is less visible at once.

Try Next twice to reach the moved block. Click a line number, then “Ask about selection.” Shift-click another line to select a range.

02 / PR as one reading surface

Review stream

Read the whole PR in flow. Bring in precision when you need it.

The appeal: stay in flow across a whole PR; inline edits are compact.
The cost: large changes need excellent loading and scroll anchoring. Before/after alignment is less immediate.

Your current leaning: mostly B, with a little A. Try Side by side, or Focus file on a file header; All files returns to the stream.

03 / Purpose as the unit of attention

Change stories

Understand what changed in behavior, with the exact code underneath.

The appeal: a large PR becomes a few understandable changes, including moves.
The cost: reliable grouping and evidence. A persuasive story must never hide code. Grouping here is authored, not AI-generated.

Open “Keep your place,” inspect the relocation, then switch to All code to check the story against the full filtered diff.

Keep what works. Mix the rest.

What feels right?

1. Does revised B have the right amount of A—side-by-side reading and temporary file focus—or is there another part of A to bring over?

2. For moved code: a compare the old/new block together, or b jump between its original and new locations?
