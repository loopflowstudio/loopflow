LOO-1078 · Vision C Surface Study · Pill vs Borderless Track

Vision C: should the track still be a pill?

Same layout, three levels of chrome. Because the workflow bar is already a 36px strip with a bottom hairline, wrapping five steps in a full outer pill can look double-boxed. Compare borderless (C1), borderless soft tray (C2), and full capsule pill (C3) across all states.

State:

C1 · No Outer Pill (Borderless Toolbar Track)

Lightest

Drop the outer capsule completely. A compact tinted ▶/⏸ button pins to the far left; the steps sit directly on the bar with only the active step highlighted (and clicking the active step or ▾ opens lifecycle/jump actions).

Why it works

No box-inside-a-box inside a 34px macOS bar. The eye goes straight to two tinted anchors: the ▶/⏸ button and the active step chip. Inactive steps read as quiet breadcrumbs until hovered.

Trade-off

Slightly softer visual grouping between ▶/⏸ and the trailing ▾, though proximity keeps them clearly one unit.

C2 · Borderless Soft Tray (6–7px macOS Control Group, No Stroke)

Balanced

Replace the round pill border with a quiet tinted 7px rounded-rect tray (like macOS Finder/Xcode segmented toolbars) — no outer hairline stroke, still clearly one connected control.

Why it works

Reads like a single native macOS toolbar group without the heavy "giant sausage pill" silhouette. Clean in both light and dark modes.

Trade-off

Adds one subtle background fill layer compared to C1.

C3 · Full Capsule Pill (Outlined)

Original C

Original Vision C with the full-height pill border and inner vertical dividers around ▶/⏸, the steps, and ▾.

Why it works

Loudest "this whole strip is a single button/control" affordance; mirrors iOS-style capsule pills.

Trade-off

Most visual weight in a 34px bar — especially on long graphs where a 450px-wide bordered pill dominates the header.
