Session tile hover controls: inset from the corner

LOO-673 Desktop polish · explore step · 2026-11-06 · built from swift/LoopflowMac/Views/WorkspacePanesView.swift (strip, PaneGeometry), swift/Loopflow/Design/DesignSystem.swift, Jack Heart's Oct 28 feedback comment.
Decision needed: how far the fix reaches. One-line padding change, one shared pane inset, or re-shaped hover chrome. Recommended: B.

Mocks are drawn at 2× from the real numbers: strip 28pt, buttons 22×20pt, corner radius 8pt. Hover a tile to reveal its controls, or . Dashed outlines are the buttons' hit frames.

A · Existing behavior

Existing. .padding(.leading, 12).padding(.trailing, 4); 4pt above the 20pt button inside a 28pt strip.

B · One pane inset, everywhere proposed · recommended

What's true: every pane edge — strip leading, strip trailing, terminal reserve, tab rows — sits one PaneGeometry.inset (= corner radius, 8pt) from the border, so nothing ever enters the corner arc.

enum PaneGeometry {
    static let cornerRadius = CornerRadius.md
    /// Chrome stays outside the corner arc on every edge.
    static let inset = cornerRadius
}

// strip
.padding(.horizontal, PaneGeometry.inset)      // was leading 12, trailing 4
// content reserve (already cornerRadius) and the stripless tab rows (.padding(4))
.padding(PaneGeometry.inset)

Cost: a handful of magic numbers become one token; the title loses 4pt of left margin (the trade against 12 on both sides, which costs 8pt of strip width). Risk: low; the stripless shell/browser tab rows move 4pt inward, worth eyeballing. A view test pins the strip's trailing inset ≥ corner radius.

C · Floating corner pill proposed

What's true: hover controls stop being part of the strip's row and become an overlay cluster that nests concentrically in the tile's corner, like window controls — and can appear on single-pane tiles, which have no strip today.

Cost: new overlay, a tinted background that must work in light and dark, title truncation must reserve the pill's width, and the hover region moves. It changes what the chrome is, which belongs in a split Task, not this bucket. Appeal: zoom/hide reachable even when a Wave shows one Session.
