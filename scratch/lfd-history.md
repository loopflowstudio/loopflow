# History of lfd and mobile connectivity

Research for Jack Heart · November 3, 2026. Historical evidence, not new direction.

Two lfd generations were removed during broader product cuts. The first already tried native iOS, launchd, Tailscale pairing and CLI delegation; its cutover remained incomplete. The second began as webhook ingress and accumulated lifecycle owners. PR #304 demonstrates divergent failure bookkeeping around a shared executor; #825 retained legacy routes; #1360 deliberately removed capabilities. History supports sharing complete operations and one lifecycle owner, not a universal server veto or a measured ranking of causes.
