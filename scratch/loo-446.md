# Strike "engine", "driver" and "lfid" (LOO-446)

Jack Heart requested this on 2026-10-09 as the cleanup after LOO-441/442/443,
stacked on LOO-443 (PR #1519) and published without landing before it. The Task
directive is accepted; the naming choices below are implementation choices made
on 2026-10-09, not further approvals. No behavior change is intended.

## Vocabulary

- LfProcess identity is `LfProcess.id` (`LfProcessId`); references are
  `<role>_lf_process_id`; `pid` is only the Unix PID. `LF_PROCESS_ID` carries it
  to children. An AgentProcess is referenced as `agent_process_id`.
- The lf process attached to a Session is the attached LfProcess
  (`attached_lf_process_id`, `SessionAttachment`); the lf process running a Flow
  is the Flow process; the executor is `flow::runner::FlowRunner`.
- The catch-all module is split: `flow/` (definition, graph, instructions,
  output, runner, transitions), `agent/` (+ `stream`), `prompt/` (+ `process`,
  `context_budget`, `structured_reply`), `skills/` (+ `catalog`, `invocation`),
  `git/` (+ `worktree`, `worktree_name`, `worktrees`), `platform/` (+ `clipboard`,
  `fs_events`), `definition/` (+ `name`), and top-level `builtins` (+ `builtins/**`),
  `config`, `error`, `command`, `naming`, `workflow`, `os_process` (+ `lifeline`),
  `terminal_title`, `machine_route`. No re-export module remains.

## Storage and wire

- Draft `lf_process_ids.sql` (depends on `agent_process`, `optional_task_pr`)
  renames the released columns, recreates `validate_flow_process_step` with its
  message, and rewrites `driver:<n>:exit` receipt keys to `attachment:<n>:exit`.
  The reader accepts only `attachment:`. History payloads are untouched.
- The two columns LOO-443's unreleased draft creates (`attached_lf_process_id`,
  `agent_sessions.agent_process_id`) are renamed in that draft, per the
  one-draft rule; everything released is renamed in this Task's draft.
- Released-schema seed SQL lives in `rust/loopflow/tests/fixtures/migrations/`,
  so old column names stay out of test sources.
- File names follow: `agent.sock`, `attachment.lifeline`.
