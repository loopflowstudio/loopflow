## Decisions and constraints

The implementation remains a headless design pending review. CLI-only omits
first-time Desktop setup; an existing release app participates in every update.
Raw promotion may reuse that app only when its helper matches the candidate;
`lf install` acquires the complete signed bundle. No helper-routing fallback,
piecemeal signed-bundle replacement, schema change or private-dev selection change.

The November 4 concept review required updates to retain the app reached by the
public CLI without the original shell environment. Explicit `LF_APPLICATIONS_DIR`
wins; otherwise use that linked bundle, then `/Applications` for initial setup.
This follows Jack Heart's coherence/custom-location outcome, not a separately
approved design. Acquisition, freshness and promotion carry one resolved target;
receipt recovery retains its pinned target before a new destination is selected.

Receipt-backed promotion remains the activation owner. Matching files still
reconcile receipts/gates before success. Only Desktop is quiesced/reopened;
Session owners and Graphs keep their images. Renamed bundles remain while used;
existing idle retirement owns eligible turnover. Preserve rollback bytes, native
identities, pending input and recovery paths.

Jack Heart's November 4 steer assigns immediate local Desktop repair to the
parent; permanent installation policy and mini rollout remain here. LOO-724 owns
retained hooks and reconnect/input recovery, and LOO-722 owns cleanup. Neither
sibling repair nor parallel cleanup/schedule changes belong to this implementation.
The accepted both-Machine verification requirement remains.

## Concept review — after destination repair, 2026-11-04

Outcome: nothing further to change in the design; no open product choice identified.
This supersedes the earlier review's request to unify destination selection.
Jack Heart's accepted coherence and preservation requirements remain unchanged;
the CLI-only first-setup interpretation remains the recorded headless assumption.

The concrete interaction is `lf install` from a shell or schedule: recover any
unfinished receipt, resolve the existing or explicit destination, acquire the
complete signed pair, then activate through promotion. `PromotionArtifacts`
carries the pair; `SwitchReceipt.activation` retains the paths for recovery.
The public link supplies ordinary destination discovery without a new location
record. Sparkle's app action remains, with bundled adoption using the same
promotion owner. Release and hotfix guidance now describe this contract.

The consequential distinction is installed selection versus running work.
Replacing Desktop does not grant authority to stop Session helpers or Graphs.
Retained images and native identities must remain usable, not merely present.
A failed acquisition/preflight preserves the prior installation; an interrupted
activation resumes the receipt's pinned target before resolving a new request.
Counterexamples that would invalidate this review are a schedule losing a custom
location, CLI-only splitting an existing pair, recovery changing destination,
or cleanup removing an image still needed by a Session. Source fixtures address
these boundaries, but do not establish configured continuity or installed rollout.

No implementation or delivery runs in this review. The next proof remains the
gate and signed, configured acceptance below, including a completed existing
Session turn with sustained interaction. The parent's interim Desktop repair
and LOO-724's reconnect work retain their separate evidence and ownership.
