## Evidence and design

summaries on October 30. They execute the absolute bundled helper with

disk cleanup. Example: native thread `f00403a0-b494-45c0-b00a-340ad021294a`,
12:35–15:16 UTC. Those historical observations do not prove the current failure.

`wave_removal` versus `task_regressed_in`; the public CLI exits 0. Neither read
alters conversations or their saved hooks.

LOO-723 owns coherent app/CLI promotion and rollout. Updating the helper at its
existing address repairs already-saved hook commands without changing native
identity, settings or history. No competing resolver, PATH fallback, database
deletion, process restart or promotion is introduced here. Coordination was
recorded on LOO-723 on November 4.

There is also a concrete retained-callback gap: saved hook settings still call
`__planning-check`, removed by `036008ec26` in favor of `__context-block --moment
stop`. Current dispatch returns success without doing the work. Keep that
published callback spelling as a hidden alias on the one ContextBlock command;
omitting the moment means Stop. Explicit moments retain their behavior.
This compatibility is required by Jack's existing-Session outcome, not a second
planning implementation. It remains while retained provider processes can emit it.

- The duplicate unknown-internal-hook bypass in `dispatch`; initial CLI handling
  remains the single retirement diagnostic. Do not add blanket error suppression.
- The no-op treatment of `__planning-check`; parse it into ContextBlock and use
  the surviving fenced native-turn/planning handler. Do not restore PlanningCheck
  or check_planning as separate command/implementation owners.
- Replace the `hooks_from_an_earlier_lf_never_block_their_provider` fixture's
  planning-no-op expectation with retained Stop execution; keep retirement
  coverage on the emitted `__session-title` callback.
- Remove the duplicate planning-no-op assertion in `global_commands`; retain

Preserve attachment fencing, subagent exclusion, native identity, existing
context sources and queued direction. Genuine failures of recognized hooks must
still return their underlying error.
