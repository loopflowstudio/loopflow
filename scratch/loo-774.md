## Implemented approach (2026-11-08)

Tag `0x05` carries JSON `{ "reason": "finished" }`, outside the counted terminal
stream. The existing `child_exited` event still carries the execution exit code;
a relay waits for Close before stopping. Unknown EOF remains unknown. The owner
flushes final output with a bounded drain so a slow viewer cannot hold execution.
Natural completion sends `finished`; lifetime policy sends `retired`. Resume and
rollover ask the old owner to send `replaced` and stop before starting its successor.
An unavailable owner retains the existing signal fallback, with no invented reason.

Existing retention judgments, control transfer and signal fallback remain.
The final frame passes through the raw relay and server bridge. Desktop's decoded
relay presents its reason and stops recovery; it does not automatically follow a
replacement. Clients can attach again by Session as documented.
