# LOO-803 observation recovery

The retry owner remains `WorkModel.keepWorkCurrent()`. Consecutive failures wait
1, 2, 4, 8, 16, then at most 30 seconds. Reset only after 30 seconds of healthy
observation, including quiet time after a successful reading. An unavailable
part interrupts health until that part recovers; unrelated updates cannot clear it.
Opening failures and ended streams share the same delay. Cancellation still
closes subscriptions and stops recovery.

test of the delay sequence, so both exercise escalation and the cap.
