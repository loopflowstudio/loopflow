# LOO-828: identity in the context block, child-agent guidance

## Outcome and acceptance

Start and compact context blocks name this Session and, when associated with
one Task, its identifier and title. A fresh Claude, Codex, OpenCode and Pi
Session can name itself without reading its environment. Identity stays out of
fixed provider instructions; tool and stop boundaries keep their existing behavior.

The `work` guidance covers harness subagents, same-checkout `lf -b` children
and separate-PR Tasks, including responsibility for results, Skill/Graph reuse,
child nesting only when requested, and waiting on exit or `lf task wait`.
`LOOPFLOW.md` changes only its Session-ID source sentence.

## Concept review — 2026-11-11

Outcome for loop-or-next: nothing to change and no open product choice within
LOO-828. Continue to the remaining checks; this review does not establish a
fresh-provider pass.

The experience stays small: read this Session's identity in context, name it,
then choose a child by the work it returns. One delivery snapshot carries
identity; the existing Session/Task association supplies it. No separate
identity service, hook-side store or child lifecycle is needed.

Recovery keeps the last saved identity when the server is unavailable; the
next successful managed start/compact refreshes it. Bare runs retain their
launch snapshot. More than one associated Task must not become a guessed
"current Task". Large reference files must not displace identity or steers.
These are checks on the existing design, not additional behavior to build.

The child brief and returned result are the handoff. Resuming finished Runs
and changing when Work guidance loads would expand the product beyond this
integration; neither is required to explain the working commands. The two
follow-up questions below remain outside this Task, not delivery decisions.

Review page: `scratch/concept-review.html`. Next show fresh-provider identity
and compaction results on the integrated build, with any account limits named.
