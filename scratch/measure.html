2026-11-11. The Machine's load average was 140–250 throughout (other agents' builds), so times are reported as alternating pairs and the claims rest on counts wherever a count exists.

Verdict

Better on both, measured on the live Machine (build ac2cba2a3). Desktop: the server's planning subscription answers in 0.6–7.8 s (median 1.3 s) instead of 18.5–39 s (median 31 s), n=5 each; current data reached Desktop 1.9, 1.9 and 3.0 s after launch at load 80–118 (before: 16.0 s, and a 15 s median over 40 earlier launches), and saved data is usable at 526–690 ms (n=8). Phone: saved list 0.89–1.0 s after process start (n=3); the Machine answers its list in 0.50–0.78 s (was a median 4.9 s). Still slow and deferred to the server Task: under concurrent subscribers the server takes 0.5–4.2 s to finish TLS and 0.4–6.6 s to send a first reading, so the phone's current list arrived 6.2 and 8.4 s after launch, and five back-to-back Desktop launches at load 165–175 took 10–34 s.
