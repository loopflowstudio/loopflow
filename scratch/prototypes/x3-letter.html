Build something you can actually ship to users.

You made something with a coding agent. It runs. You showed it to a friend and it mostly worked.

Then you asked for one more change and three other things broke. Or the working version is somewhere in a chat you can’t find. Or it is fine on your laptop and you would not dare let a stranger sign up.

The distance between “it runs” and “people use it” is the whole job of a software engineer.

Engineers don’t get there by writing better prompts. They get there by working in a certain order:

  • Decide what you are building before anyone builds it.
  • Change one thing at a time, somewhere it can’t break the rest.
  • Check it. Then look at it running.
  • Only then let it in.

Loopflow is that order, for you and the coding agent you already have. We’ll get you building whatever software you want like a software engineer, by guiding you through workflows that get the most out of you and ensure high quality software, not slop.

It is a Mac app. Your code stays in your GitHub and on your machine. It is free and open source, and we use it to build itself.
