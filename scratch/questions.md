  the reversible naming choice. Human names win; semantic refinement is separate.
- Native hooks are distributed during published installation. Jack's settings and
  accounts were not changed. Codex requires its ordinary hook trust review.
- Account-free probes establish Codex live TUI adoption through its shared server.
  Embedded launches expose no such endpoint, and an already-loaded unnamed thread
  does not invoke SessionStart when its TUI reconnects. These are counterexamples
  to universal hook-only naming, not missing screenshots. A supported embedded and
  attach mechanism remains to be designed; native storage writes and competing
  OSC writers remain excluded.
- cmux workspace/tab renames do not emit another OSC title. Window-bar propagation
  needs host evidence and a supported repair if it fails. cmux denied separate
  workspace creation before making changes; existing windows remain untouched.
