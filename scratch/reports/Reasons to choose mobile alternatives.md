# Make mobile work worth returning to

Research for Jack Heart · November 3, 2026. Historical evidence, not new direction.

Other products make catch-up, visual context, review, notifications and originating work easier to discover. These are reasons someone may prefer them even with a good terminal connection. Voice composition is not spoken response; local/cloud and terminal/native-chat surfaces differ. This research did not expand accepted scope or measure product quality. Evaluate a long edited instruction, a short catch-up and interruption/recovery during device review.
