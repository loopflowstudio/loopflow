# Reasons to prefer Paseo mobile

Research for Jack Heart · November 3, 2026. Historical evidence, not new direction.

Paseo supports readable catch-up, files/diffs, image picking, voice, notifications and workspace creation. Host-side speech is not offline iPhone recognition; browser is Desktop-only. Relay simplifies setup while adding a service dependency. Release claims and source tests are not measured mobile quality. No feature scope changed.
