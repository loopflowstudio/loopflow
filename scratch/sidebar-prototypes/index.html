Different strengths, rather than three visual styles.

01 / NOTIONOfficial reference ↗

A container is
also a place.

Notion nests pages inside pages. Parents and children have the same capabilities, so an organizing node can hold useful content of its own.

Borrow: let the repo and every Wave be full destinations, with summaries and a real selected state.

Leave behind: an arrow and another indent at every level.

02 / WORKFLOWYOfficial reference ↗

Depth without
shrinking the view.

Clicking a bullet zooms into that node. Breadcrumbs keep its ancestry accessible while the selected branch takes the whole view.

Borrow: let a Wave become the sidebar’s scope. Give its Tasks the full width, with a short path back.

Repo / Wave→Wave
Tasks, at full width

Tradeoff: focus improves; simultaneous overview decreases.

03 / FIGMAOfficial reference ↗

Make the structure
feel spatial.

Layers correspond to visible canvas objects. Hover connects the two; collapsing can preserve the selected branch. Nesting becomes something you can locate.

Borrow: give parent context and selected work distinct visual roles and stable positions.

Three working prototypes

Same fictional work. Three different navigation models.

01 / Keep the whole map

Continuous outline

One connected rail. Waves read like chapter headings; Tasks get room for a title, next step and status.

Gains: continuity and overview.
Costs: vertical space.

Explore outline ↗
02 / Move into the work

Focus sidebar

Open a Wave to fill the sidebar with its Tasks. Clickable ancestors replace the staircase of indentation.

Gains: focus and generous detail.
Costs: a step back to sibling Waves.

Explore focus ↗
03 / Give every level a place

Wave / Task navigator

Waves form a persistent vertical index. Switching Waves restores the last Task, its draft and scroll position. No folding required.

Gains: fast lateral switching.
Costs: a wider sidebar.
