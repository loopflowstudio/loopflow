Loopflow theme catalogue

A proposal for 12 Light and 12 Dark themes. Each assignment picker shows only its own 12: Loopflow Light and Linen lead the light side, Loopflow Dark and Hearth the dark side, and 10 companions fill each. All candidates come from the Ghostty catalogue (iTerm2-Color-Schemes, 812 themes, commit 21f281d). Five current companions stay and fifteen are new. Every theme has a license that clearly permits redistribution; anything unclear is excluded. Popular names stay where reuse is permitted; everything else takes a one-word material name like Linen and Hearth.

Background spread

each block is one theme's terminal background, sorted by lightness then hue

The current darks bunch at blue-violet: 9 of 12 sit between hue 258° and 291°. The proposed dark side keeps one of those (Mocha) and adds rose, purple, phosphor green, olive, teal, navy and true black. The lights gain yellow, tan, lilac, mint, blue and cool gray beside white. The light pool is thinner: only about 70 of 812 lights read well, and most are plain white.
