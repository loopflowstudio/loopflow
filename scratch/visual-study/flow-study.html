Make the next action understandable

These references separate the overview from step details. We’re adapting that pattern to the moment before a Task’s first run. The diagrams show order and nested flows; they do not invent parallelism or completed steps.

Affordances worth carrying forward

  • Show the selected Flow and its order before Start.
  • Let someone inspect a step without opening an editor.
  • Keep Flow changes one level deeper; update the preview immediately.
  • Make human approval steps explicit where the Flow actually declares them.
  • Keep comments separate from the current description and collapsed by default.
