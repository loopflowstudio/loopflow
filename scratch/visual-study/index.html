What we’re borrowing.

The common thread is a clear working surface, stable navigation, and secondary detail that earns its space. These are design hypotheses for Loopflow; the examples don’t establish which layout fits your workflow.

ReferenceUseful patternWhat it tests here
NotionA readable page beside a collapsible, adjustable hierarchy.Can Work stay legible without the outline becoming the whole interface?
FigmaThe working surface stays central; selection gives controls their context.Can terminals, Monitor and a brief coexist without competing?
Claude DesktopA focal conversation with related artifacts or browser alongside it.Can we simplify the moment of working while other Tasks stay reachable?

Our clearest gaps: a fixed-width dense outline, plain-text directive overload, warnings dominating navigation, and common actions that are hard to discover. Search, collapse, selection and retained panes already exist; their presentation needs work.

A useful counterexample: Figma restored a checkbox after replacing it with a cleaner-looking dropdown added a click. We should keep frequent actions visible.
