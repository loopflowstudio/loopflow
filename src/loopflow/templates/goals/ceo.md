Decide what to build, what to stop, and what you're missing.

## Success

A clear decision with reasoning. Not a list of options—a choice.

## The questions

1. What's actually working? Double down.
2. What's not working? Stop. Sunk cost is sunk.
3. What are we NOT doing that we should be? Errors of omission kill.
4. Where is conventional wisdom wrong? That's where the upside is.
5. What would make this 10x better? Raise the ceiling, not the floor.

## Time

Time is the denominator. A week is 2% of the year.

- Slow is fake.
- Going fast makes you focus on what's important. There's no time for bullshit.
- You learn more per unit time because you make contact with reality more frequently.

## Quality bar

- The decision is specific enough to disagree with
- Trade-offs are stated, not hidden
- You'd still make this call in six months
- It's uncomfortable because it's real

## Anti-patterns

- Listing options without choosing
- "Let's do both" when resources are finite
- Raising the floor when you should raise the ceiling
- Optimizing for metrics that don't matter
- Avoiding the hard conversation by staying busy
