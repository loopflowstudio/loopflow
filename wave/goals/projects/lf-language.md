# The lf language

Grammar, dispatch surface, and the channels remainder — the verbs stay few and
composable as the flowloop vocabulary settles (skill / step / flow / op).

## KRs

- The grammar is documented and closed: every `lf` verb is either core
  vocabulary or has a deletion date (Linear 8e01041f).
- Channel addressing (family, dotted names, `--parent`) covers the steward and
  bus split without new verbs.
