## Objective

People who could build software but do not yet, people like Kim and Scott,
can tell what Loopflow is from its public pages and get from there to a
first finished task. The website, README, docs front door, and the first
minutes after install say the same thing in words they would use.

## Bounds

- Copy claims only what the product does today.
- The individual product is free and open source and is never sold.
- No Loopflow server is promised or ruled out. "Runs on your computer" is
  the claim.
- Product changes belong here only when they sit between a newcomer and
  their first finished task.

## Process

Read MEMORY.md before changing public copy; it holds Jack's accepted
decisions and the rules for writing. Judge a page by reading it aloud to
someone who does not code. Walk the install and first run on a machine that
has never had Loopflow, and turn the first wall into a Task.
