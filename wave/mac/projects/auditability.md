# Auditability

Overseeing: seeing everything that's going on, unabridged. The steward's
curation is a reading aid, never a filter on the record.

## KRs

- The agent bus gets its own audit window beside runs: raw `lf chat`
  traffic (worker reports, escalations, flowloop outcomes), full history,
  no curation.
- Runs are drillable: from the runs/op-frame motion view into one run's
  detail, and from a live run to its attachable session (execs are
  attachable — grab the wheel).
- The wave list ranks by attention (failed → waiting → running → idle) and
  every wave carries its reason; state, branch/PR context, and terminal
  status are visible around every live session.
